## 1. Story Goal

The cheapest gesture and the one a four-year-old tries first: touch the thing and it answers. Modes
`any`, `count_n` and `toggle`, shipped through `t21_touch_each`, where every object answers and no order is
required.

## 2. Requirements

#### ADDED: Tap, in three modes
**After:** `any`, `count_n` (distinct targets) and `toggle`, built on `TapButton` with its squash feedback, one finger per target and the `device == -1` echo filter.

#### ADDED: t21_touch_each
**After:** Every object answers its own touch; a spent target replays its reaction without changing progress; no auto-end timer (`AddAutoEndTimer` has no counterpart), so the step ends on the child's effort.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; lint/format pass; bars hold.
2. **Built on `TapButton`:** squash-spring feedback, one finger per target, `device == -1` echo filtering; the primitive adds no second tap surface.
3. **Three modes:** `any` (one of the targets is enough), `count_n` (any n distinct targets), `toggle` (a target's state flips and persists for the step).
4. **Every object answers:** in `t21_touch_each` each target plays its own reaction and marks itself; a second tap on a spent target replays its reaction without changing progress.
5. **No auto-end timer:** the TutoTOONS scene calls `AddAutoEndTimer`; this template deliberately has none (design page, adaptation rules). A step ends on the child's effort.
6. **Instant answer:** reaction and SFX in the same frame as the touch.

## 4. Zoom-Out on the Modules This Touches

**`TouchDraggable`** — purpose: be the only drag surface in the game: one finger, four modes, echo filtering.

- Callers: 29 files including 17 legacy scene scripts and four test scripts.
- Contracts to preserve: 326 lines, grandfathered by INV-09 — it may not grow. The Tap primitive configures it; it never subclasses or edits it.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### A spent target still answers

Progress does not change, but the tap still reacts — a dead control teaches a child that touching does not work.

### Counting is distinct targets

`count_n` never counts the same target twice, matching `t21`'s `isCorrect` bookkeeping.

### No text on a target

Targets are pictures; a label is an icon and, if voiced, a key.

## 8. Out of Scope

- Pairs and sequence tapping (e15 modifiers, e15s02).
- Moving targets (`t37`, wave 3).

## 9. Risks

- **Counting the same target twice** inflates progress and finishes a step nobody finished. Mitigation: a test taps the same object n+1 times.
- **A tap target that dies after one use** teaches a child that touching stops working. Mitigation: the replay test is part of the story, not a nicety.
- **Legacy `TapButton` consumers** (36 files) must not change behaviour. Mitigation: the primitive composes, never edits.
