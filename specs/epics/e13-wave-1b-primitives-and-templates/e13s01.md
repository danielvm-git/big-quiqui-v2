## 1. Story Goal

The second gesture: move a tool over an area and let the covered surface answer. Three modes — clear,
leave and reveal — with reveal shipped here through `t34_rub_reveal`, so the primitive arrives with a
template that proves it end to end.

## 2. Requirements

#### ADDED: Rub, in three modes
**After:** `clear`, `leave` and `reveal` on top of `ScrubGesture` + `TouchDraggable.CONTINUOUS_SCRUB`. Progress is the covered-area ratio reported to the runner; the tool stays in the hand while the finger moves; one loop SFX channel per gesture.

#### ADDED: t34_rub_reveal
**After:** Layers, their order and their reveal reaction come from the step def; each layer animates away as it is rubbed. The template knows no room.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; lint/format pass; no file over 300 lines, no function over 20.
2. **Reuse the hull, do not re-derive it:** the primitive consumes `ScrubGesture` (hull test, point count, the one loop channel) and `TouchDraggable`'s `CONTINUOUS_SCRUB`; `scrub_gesture.gd` owns the counting.
3. **Three modes:** `clear` (remove coverage), `leave` (add coverage), `reveal` (each layer animates away as it is rubbed). Progress is the ratio of covered area, reported to the runner rather than asserting completion itself.
4. **The tool stays in the hand:** the finger moves and the tool does not, one loop SFX channel for the gesture, and the tool returns home when the finger lifts.
5. **t34_rub_reveal works from data:** layers, their order and their reveal reactions come from the `StepDef`; the template knows no room.
6. **Instant answer:** the first valid rub point plays the loop start within the same frame; a rub outside the hull does nothing and is not an error.
7. **Both aspects:** the hull is transformed at call time from `scrub_hull_global()`, so 4:3 and 16:9 agree.

## 4. Zoom-Out on the Modules This Touches

**`ScrubGesture`** — purpose: own the hull test, the point count and the single loop channel of a continuous gesture.

- Callers: `touch_draggable` (CONTINUOUS_SCRUB) and the Rub primitive.
- Contracts to preserve: `begin(host)`/`consume(point)`/`stop()`/`hull_points()` stay; a missing or freed target means zero progress, never an error; one loop channel per gesture.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### No hull, no scrubs

A target that is missing, freed, not in the tree or without `scrub_hull_global()` means zero progress — never an error and never a stuck state.

### One loop channel

A gesture owns at most one looping SFX; `ScrubGesture.stop()` is the only way it ends.

### Reveal is irreversible, clear is not

Reveal layers do not come back inside the step; clear coverage stays cleared. Both are data flags, not template branches.

## 8. Out of Scope

- `clear` and `leave` in a shipped room (e13s04 brings t23/t40).
- Erase/draw completion checks beyond ratio (t23's own progress rules land with it).

## 9. Risks

- **A hull read from a moving rig** yields a stale area and progress that never reaches the threshold. Mitigation: `hull_points()` transforms at call time and a test rubs after a rig move.
- **A loop SFX that outlives the gesture** keeps playing for the whole scene. Mitigation: `stop()` is the only exit and a test asserts the channel count returns to zero.
- **Reveal layered over a mouse-echo event** would double-count. Mitigation: runs under `TouchSim`, which injects the echo deliberately.
