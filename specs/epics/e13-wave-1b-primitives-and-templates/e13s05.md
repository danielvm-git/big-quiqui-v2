## 1. Story Goal

The three templates that wrap a session: the start screen, the dressing/decoration screen with a
scrolling category list, and the reward collection at the end of a cycle.

## 2. Requirements

#### ADDED: t45_dress_decorate
**After:** A scrolling category strip whose items snap onto slots or free-place, saved through `ProgressManager.equip`/`place_furniture`. The bin returns an item with a soft sound and never opens a confirm dialog.

#### ADDED: t0_title and t32_reward
**After:** The title screen has exactly one interactive target; the reward screen pays once through the existing cycle guard and shows the star already lit on re-entry. The `T39` gift that was gated behind an ad is simply free.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; lint/format pass; bars hold.
2. **`t45_dress_decorate`:** a scrolling category strip (`ScrollView` + `CategoryController` equivalents) whose items snap onto slots or free place, with the look saved through `ProgressManager.equip`/`place_furniture`. The bin (`ShowBin`) returns an item with a soft sound, never a confirm dialog.
3. **`t0_title`:** one glowing target starts the game; no policy link, no subscription, no text (`StartSceneController` maps to a single tap action).
4. **`t32_reward`:** the reward is collected with a tap, coins fly to the HUD, the star appears, and the gift `T39` gated behind an ad is simply free.
5. **Rewards are paid once:** payment goes through `CareLoopManager`/`ProgressManager`'s existing once-per-cycle guard; re-entering the reward screen after payment shows the star already lit.
6. **No ad path anywhere:** the templates have no branch that can show, offer or simulate an ad.

## 4. Zoom-Out on the Modules This Touches

**`ProgressManager`** — purpose: own the save, the wallet, the equipped look, the placed furniture and the stars.

- Callers: Every scene, `CoinTicketHud`, `CareLoopManager`, `test_*_progress*`.
- Contracts to preserve: `equip`/`equipped`/`place_furniture`/`placed`/`add_star`/`add_coins` keep their signatures and the atomic `.tmp` write; any new field is a `SaveData` version bump with a migration, never a shape change.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### Categories are data

The strip's categories, their items and their slots live in the def; `EconomyData` keeps prices out of the template.

### The title screen has one verb

Tap the glowing thing. Anything else on that screen is decoration with `MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE`.

### The reward is payment, not gameplay

The template displays and triggers; the amount and the once-only rule belong to the services.

## 9. Risks

- **A second payment on re-entry** duplicates coins and tickets. Mitigation: the existing cycle guard plus a test that enters the reward screen twice.
- **Slots that save the wrong id** corrupt the child's look. Mitigation: ids validated with `Ids.PATTERN` before any save call, and a test with an invalid id.
- **The title screen growing a second button** (parent area, policy link) is the TutoTOONS pattern the scope declined. Mitigation: the one-target test.
