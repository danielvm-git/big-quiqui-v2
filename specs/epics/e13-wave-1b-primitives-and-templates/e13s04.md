## 1. Story Goal

The cleaning mechanic the bathroom depends on: `t40_draw_fill` adds coverage until the area is done, and
`t23_erase` removes coverage until it is clear. Together they replace the eight hand-placed foam patches and
the two hand-wired rinse/dry stages.

## 2. Requirements

#### ADDED: t40_draw_fill
**After:** A tool paints a layer until the def's completion threshold (e.g. 0.9) is reached; the layer visual, the tool and the SFX come from the def; disabling the tray hides nothing, it only stops accepting taps.

#### ADDED: t23_erase
**After:** A tool clears a layer until it is gone, then `CheckProgress` completes once. Erase radius and sound come from the def; residue is allowed and the result still looks clean on exit.

#### MODIFIED: How the bathroom cleans
**Before:** Eight hand-placed `FoamBlob` nodes scrubbed inside the rig hull, plus two hand-wired rinse and dry stages in `banheiro.gd`.
**After:** Foam and water become layers with a measured ratio, so the same two templates serve soap, rinse and dry as three steps of one def. The legacy scene keeps its behaviour until e14 migrates it.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; lint/format pass; bars hold.
2. **Progress is measured, not stepped:** both templates report covered/cleared ratio from the Rub primitive; the completion threshold is a def value (e.g. 0.9), not a hardcoded 100%.
3. **`t40_draw_fill`:** a tool paints a layer until the threshold is reached; the layer's visual, the tool and the SFX come from the def. `DisableTools` maps to disabling the tray without hiding it.
4. **`t23_erase`:** a tool clears a layer until it is gone; the erase radius and the sound come from the def. Completion is `CheckProgress`, exactly like the TutoTOONS template.
5. **No punished imperfection:** stopping at 0.9 completes the step; leaving residue is allowed and looks clean on exit.
6. **A step can chain them:** the bathroom's soap→rinse→dry is three steps in one def, each one template, no scene script.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### Two templates, no bathroom nouns

`foam`, `water` and `towel` are def values. The templates never learn the room's name.

### Erase never removes progress already credited

Once a step completes, later rubbing is free play and cannot un-complete it.

### The tray never fights the finger

While a rub gesture owns the screen, the tray ignores its own taps.

## 9. Risks

- **Progress that stalls just under the threshold** leaves a child rubbing a clean surface. Mitigation: the threshold is data (0.9), and a test asserts completion at it.
- **Erase un-completing a finished step** would take a reward away. Mitigation: a test rubs after completion and asserts the step stays done.
- **The tray fighting the finger** during a rub. Mitigation: while a rub owns the screen the tray ignores its own taps, asserted under `TouchSim`.
