## 1. Story Goal

A world wider than the screen, dragged with one finger, with destinations that open by playing and never
by paying. `t25_map` is what turns the house hub and the village into data instead of two hand-built
screens.

## 2. Requirements

#### ADDED: Pan with drag/follow and a tap tolerance
**After:** One finger drags a world wider than the canvas with edge clamping; a drag past the tolerance cancels the tap it started on; `follow` auto-scrolls to a target when the room opens.

#### ADDED: t25_map
**After:** Destinations are a table in the def — id, world position, icon, lock rule and the scene id it opens. Locks read the care loop only: a locked door dims, refuses with a soft sound and points at the next care step. No price, no payment, no photo gallery, no download branch.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; lint/format pass; bars hold.
2. **Pan modes:** `drag` (follow the finger with edge clamping) and `follow` (auto-scroll to a target, used when the room opens). One finger at a time; a pan never starts on a tap target.
3. **Locks open by play:** a locked destination shows, dims and refuses politely (a soft sound plus a wish bubble pointing at the next care step). It never shows a price and never opens with money.
4. **`t25_map` from data:** destinations are a table in the `StepDef` — id, position on the world, icon, lock rule, and the scene id it opens.
5. **Pan and tap coexist:** a drag that exceeds the tap tolerance cancels the tap, so a child panning never opens a room by accident.
6. **Destinations come from the catalogue (F-3):** every destination resolves through `SceneCatalog`. A def
   value that is not in the catalogue is rejected and logged; no scene path is ever concatenated from a def
   string (`SC-e13s03-P0-05`).
7. **Both aspects:** the world's size and the clamp come from the def, not from screen pixels; verified at `1920×1080` and `2048×1536`.

## 4. Zoom-Out on the Modules This Touches

**`PoiTable / FriendCatalog`** — purpose: hold the Vila points of interest and the friend minigame targets.

- Callers: `vila.gd`, `festa.gd`, `album.gd` (legacy) and the new map template.
- Contracts to preserve: The POI ids are stable strings; while legacy scenes exist the table must still answer with a `res://` path.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### A locked door is never a scolding

Refusal is a sound and a picture. No text, no shake, no penalty.

### No payment path

`t25_map` in TutoTOONS loads purchase and photo-gallery branches (`ShowOrHidePhotoGallery`); both are out — the locks read the care loop, nothing else.

### Pan is not a scrollbar

There is no visible scrollbar or arrow; the camera follows the finger.

## 8. Out of Scope

- Migrating the casa and vila screens onto the map (e14).
- Photo gallery, rooms downloads, followers (declined in scope).

## 9. Risks

- **A pan that fires a door tap** sends a child into the wrong room. Mitigation: the tolerance test, with the exact pixel threshold in the def.
- **Locks that read money** would reintroduce the payment path the scope declined. Mitigation: a source-scan test asserting the template never touches `ProgressManager.get_coins`.
- **A world size in pixels** breaks 4:3. Mitigation: world size and clamp come from the def; `GeometryOracle` asserts both aspects.

## Verification Script

1. `bash scripts/preflight.sh` → PASS.
2. Open the map def and drag the world left and right → it clamps at both edges.
3. Tap a destination → it opens; drag across a destination → nothing opens.
4. Tap a locked destination → it dims, plays a soft sound and the wish bubble points at the next care step.
5. Relaunch at 4:3 → no bar, and the same destinations are reachable.
