## 1. Story Goal
Prove the closet the way a child plays it: the hub's dress door, a drag onto her body, her face still on
top afterwards, the loop moved to PLAY, and the look written down so a **new visit** opens already
dressed — on the iPad canvas too, because that is where a layer inversion would be invisible.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`.
2. **Explicit State Machine:** the journey runs with a clean `FsmProbe` on the care loop and on the scene
   manager; a replay never double-credits DRESS.
3. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** every gesture is a `TouchSim.drag_events`/`tap_events` sequence with the
   echo present.
4. **Dual-Aspect Responsive Layout:** the journey is asserted at `1920x1080` and `2048x1536`, one canvas
   per test.
5. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.
6. **The journey:** dress door → closet → equip outfit + accessory → `credited` → loop in PLAY → saved →
   Casa requested once.
7. **The invariant:** `expression.z_index > outfit.z_index` and the expression visible, on both canvases.
8. **Disk round-trip:** a fresh mount of the closet shows the saved outfit and accessory.
9. **Replay:** equipping the same item again increments the child's action count but never the credit.

## 3. Frozen Contract
`game/tests/integration/test_closet_e2e.gd` — one test per canvas (`_assert_journey(size)`), because the
loop and the SceneManager are singletons and a loop over two canvases carries state between mounts (the
first draft did exactly that and lied about the second canvas).

## 4. Test Scenarios
- `SC-E05-P0-20` the journey at `1920x1080`
- `SC-E05-P0-21` the journey at `2048x1536`
- `SC-E05-P0-22` the look survives leaving and returning (fresh mount, iPad canvas)
- `SC-E05-P1-23` replaying the same outfit credits nothing more
- `SC-E05-P1-24` leaving without dressing keeps the previous look
