# e07s01: Playable Scene 7: SleepScene — lamp toggle, night tint, plush tuck, zzz, SLEEP credit

<!-- story: e07s01 -->
- **Epic:** `e07-sleep-and-celebration-scenes`
- **BCP:** 4
- **Playable Vertical Target:** `res://scenes/quarto_sono.tscn` + `res://scenes/celebracao.tscn`
- **Threat findings closed here:** T-E07-01 (lamp state), T-E07-02 (SLEEP credited once)

## 1. Story Goal
The bedroom at night: one tap on the lamp dims the room and her face turns sleepy, dragging the
plush onto her tucks her in, zzz particles rise, and SLEEP is credited once — then the celebration is
requested.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`, type gate passes.
2. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** every interactive node filters `device == -1`; one tap or drag is one action.
3. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.
1. Two interactions complete the step: **lamp toggle** (`LampButton`, tap) then **tuck** (`Plush`, `SNAP_TO_SLOT` onto the rig). Order is free: either first, both required.
2. The lamp has two states (lit/dim) with a night tint applied to the background only, never to the rig (a dark character is a broken character).
3. The tuck sets the `sono` expression and starts the `zzz` particles; it is idempotent (a second tuck does not restart the burst).
4. SLEEP is credited exactly once and only when the loop is in SLEEP; `CELEBRATE` is requested once afterwards.
5. `HomeButton` abandons an uncredited SLEEP step and stops the particles.
6. `GeometryOracle` with `STEP_ROOM_SPEC` passes at 1920x1080 and 2048x1536; no text in the scene.
