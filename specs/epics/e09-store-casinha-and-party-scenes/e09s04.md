## Story Goal
The epic's own claim — "the room looks the same on an iPad and on a desktop" — becomes an *oracle*
instead of a sentence. One test walks the real economy end to end (buy two pieces in the store, place
them in the casinha, leave, come back, and re-mount the same save on the other canvas) and fails if a
piece lands more than 2% of the room away from where the child left it.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`.
2. **Explicit State Machine:** the oracle asserts on real `Fsm` states (store `SOLD`, casinha
   `DRESSED`, party `DANCING`), never on private flags.
3. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** the walk drives every interaction through `TouchSim` with
   `with_mouse_echo = true`.
4. **Dual-Aspect Responsive Layout:** `GeometryOracle` passes for all three scenes at `1920x1080`,
   `2048x1536` and the expanded `1920x1440` 4:3 canvas.
5. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.

### Normalized oracle
6. `PlacementOracle.audit_normalized(canvas_a: Vector2i, canvas_b: Vector2i, save_after_a: Array[Dictionary],
   mounted_b: Control, area_name: String) -> Array[String]` returns findings when a saved `(id, x, y)`
   is not restored within `RATIO_TOLERANCE = 0.02` of the room on the second canvas, or when a prop is
   missing, or when a saved coordinate falls outside `[0,1]`.
7. The oracle reads the prop's **painted** rect centre (`get_global_rect()`), not its anchor box, the same
   discipline as `GeometryOracle` after BUG-013.
8. A coordinate saved on 16:9 and one saved on 4:3 are interchangeable: placing at the same finger ratio
   on both canvases yields the same saved `(x, y)` within `RATIO_TOLERANCE`.

### Epic walk
9. `test_e09_epic_e2e.gd` covers, on one save: `mercado` buy → coins decreased → `casinha` place two
   pieces → `placed_count() == 2` → remount on the other canvas → both props restored at the same ratio →
   `festa` still `LOCKED` at two placed pieces (the threshold is real, not asserted in isolation).
10. The walk asserts the diary: exactly one `store.buy` per purchase and one `casinha.place` per
    placement (no duplicated events from the mouse echo).

## Test Scenarios
- `SC-E09-P0-01` a placement saved on 16:9 restores within 2% on the 4:3 iPad canvas
- `SC-E09-P0-02` the same finger ratio on both canvases saves the same normalized coordinate
- `SC-E09-P0-03` a saved coordinate outside `[0,1]` (tampered save) is clamped, not crashed
- `SC-E09-P0-04` the epic walk: buy → place → reload → party still locked → exactly one event each
- `SC-E09-P1-05` all three scenes are oracle-clean on all three canvases
- `SC-E09-P2-06` the oracle itself fails when a coordinate is moved by 10% (a test that can fail)

## Risks
- **A green oracle that cannot fail** (the e08 lesson, BUG-040): `SC-E09-P2-06` mutates a saved coordinate
  and asserts the oracle reports a finding.
