## Story Goal
The first **free placement** verb. She drags a piece of furniture she owns from the tray into the room
and it stays where she let go — on any canvas. The position is stored normalized (`0..1` of the room),
so the same room looks identical on the iPad 4:3 canvas and on a 16:9 desktop canvas. Nothing is
hardcoded in pixels, so nothing drifts when the canvas expands.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`.
2. **Explicit State Machine:** `CasinhaScene` uses the shared `TouchDraggable` `Fsm` for the drag and a
   table-driven `Fsm` for the room (`EMPTY -> DRESSED`); zero `illegal_transition` / `dwell_timeout`.
3. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** one drag from `TouchSim.drag_events(..., with_mouse_echo=true)` moves
   the prop exactly once (the synthesized mouse echo never doubles the gesture).
4. **Dual-Aspect Responsive Layout:** `GeometryOracle` passes at `1920x1080` and `2048x1536`, zero
   letterbox, `>=120x120 px` hit targets for every draggable prop.
5. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.

### TouchDraggable FREE_PLACEMENT (MODIFIED — additive, other modes unchanged)
6. In `FREE_PLACEMENT` mode a drop inside `placement_area` **keeps** the new position (`REST` at the
   dropped point) and emits `placed(normalized: Vector2)`; a drop outside the area returns home like
   every other mode and emits nothing.
7. `normalized_of(global_point) -> Vector2` clamps to `[0,1]` and is portrait/landscape independent: it
   is a ratio of the placement area's rect, never a pixel value.
8. `CONSUME_ON_DROP`, `CONTINUOUS_SCRUB` and `SNAP_TO_SLOT` behaviour, signals and tests are unchanged;
   the file stays within its grandfathered size (it is not allowed to grow past 333 lines, and the
   helper extraction below must leave it smaller than it was).
9. `CasinhaScene` may disable placement with `enabled = false` on a not-owned prop; a disabled prop never
   transitions (`can_transition_to` guard), so a locked tray cannot wedge the single-finger lock.

### Normalized persistence (MODIFIED)
10. `ProgressManager.place_furniture(id, nx, ny) -> bool` upserts one entry in
    `SaveData.placed_furniture`, clamps to `[0,1]`, requires the id to be valid **and owned**, and writes
    once (atomic). `remove_furniture(id) -> bool` clears one entry (a piece cannot be both in the tray
    and the room). `placed_count() -> int` and `placed() -> Array[Dictionary]` are the read side.
11. A placement survives a reload; `SaveData._clean_placement` already drops malformed entries and
    clamps out-of-range coordinates (existing contract, re-asserted here).

### CasinhaScene (ADDED — `res://scenes/casinha.tscn`)
12. Background `bg_casinha`, `QuiQuiRig`, `CoinTicketHud`, `HomeButton`, a `PlacementArea` (the room),
    a `Tray` with up to four owned pieces (`FurnitureProp` nodes wrapping `TouchDraggable`), and a
    `HintLayer`/`HintManager` reusing the e08 hint component to teach the first drag (FIX-02).
13. On mount: every saved placement is restored at its normalized coordinates; the tray holds owned but
    unplaced pieces only. No saved placements means the room is empty, not broken.
14. Dropping a piece in the room persists `(id, nx, ny)` and removes it from the tray; a piece the child
    never bought is not in the tray at all.
15. `HomeButton` returns to `casa.tscn`; the scene closes a dangling `CELEBRATE` on arrival.
16. The Vila's `CASINHA` door navigates to `casinha.tscn`.

## Test Scenarios
- `SC-E09-P0-01` `normalized_of` maps a canvas-independent ratio; clamped at both edges
- `SC-E09-P0-02` a FREE_PLACEMENT drop inside the room keeps the position and emits `placed` once
- `SC-E09-P0-03` a drop outside the room flies home and emits nothing
- `SC-E09-P0-04` the casinha boots clean on both aspects with an empty save (empty room, full tray)
- `SC-E09-P0-05` a drag + drop persists `(id, nx, ny)` and a remount restores it at the same ratio
- `SC-E09-P0-06` an owned-but-placed piece is not in the tray; an unowned piece is in neither
- `SC-E09-P1-07` CONSUME_ON_DROP / SNAP_TO_SLOT / CONTINUOUS_SCRUB suites stay green (no regression)
- `SC-E09-P1-08` the Vila `CASINHA` door navigates

## Risks
- **`touch_draggable.gd` is grandfathered debt (331 lines).** The change must *shrink* it: extract the
  duplicated target-rect maths into one helper and route both existing callers through it.
- **Placement vs. canvas resize:** the prop must be re-laid-out from the normalized value on
  `resized`/remount, never from a stale pixel position.
