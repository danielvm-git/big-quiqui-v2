## 1. Story Goal

A child who stops moving gets taught, not stuck. After a few seconds with no input the ghost hand shows
the gesture; any touch stops it. The lesson is visual and voiced — there is no text.

## 2. Requirements

#### MODIFIED: Who decides when a hint appears
**Before:** Each of the three legacy scenes that teach a drag (`urso_puzzle`, `casinha`, `vila`) enqueues a `DragHint` from its own script on its own schedule, with the hand's from/to hardcoded in that scene.
**After:** The step def declares the idle threshold and the gesture's source/target; the player watches the input clock and the shared `HintManager` queue owns the timing. Any touch cancels the hand, the cooldown prevents a second pop, and the legacy scenes keep their behaviour until e14 migrates them.

#### ADDED: Hint content is data
**After:** `hint: { delay_sec: 3.0, from: <node id>, to: <node id>, voice: vo.<scene>.hint }` in the step def. A def without a hint block simply has no hint.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; lint/format pass; bars hold.
2. **One hint at a time:** `HintManager`'s queue and cooldown are reused as they are (they already mirror TutoTOONS' `HintManager` + `BeginCoolDown`).
3. **Trigger is data:** the idle threshold lives in the step def (default ~3 s) and the hint describes that step's own gesture — source rect and target rect come from the def, never from hardcoded pixels.
4. **Any touch cancels it:** the first input of any kind hides the hand immediately, and the cooldown prevents it from popping straight back.
5. **No hint loops forever:** a step that never gets input shows one hint per cooldown window; there is no timer that ends the step.
6. **Node references are declared ids, not paths (F-3):** `from`/`to` resolve through a table the player registers at load. A value that walks out of the room (`..`, `../`, `../../root/...`) is rejected and logged, never passed to `get_node` (`SC-e12s05-P1-04`).
7. **Picture-only:** nothing a child must read. If the def supplies a voice key it plays once per hint; a missing key is silent, not an error.

## 4. Zoom-Out on the Modules This Touches

**`HintManager`** — purpose: show one hint at a time with a queue and a cooldown.

- Callers: `urso_puzzle`, `casinha`, `vila` (legacy) and now the player.
- Contracts to preserve: `enqueue`, `try_show_next`, `stop_current`, `begin_cooldown`, `advance` stay; the hint object contract is `visible`, `stop()`, `advance(dt)`, optional `done()`.

**`DragHint`** — purpose: the ghost hand itself: slide a hand from a source to a target and pulse the target.

- Callers: `HintManager` consumers only.
- Contracts to preserve: `play(from_global, to_global, target_pulse)` keeps its signature; `MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE` is non-negotiable (a hint must never solve the step); `manual_step` stays for tests.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### The hand teaches, it does not play

The ghost hand is `MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE` and can never solve the step.

### Hints are per step, not per scene

Entering a step resets the idle clock; leaving it hides the hand.

### The placeholder stays replaceable

Today `DragHint` draws a rectangle. It must take rects from data so the real hand art drops in without touching the player.

## 9. Risks

- **A hint that fires while the child is playing** is an insult and a distraction. Mitigation: the idle clock resets on any input, including a pointer move.
- **The placeholder rectangle becomes permanent.** Mitigation: the rects come from data, so real hand art is an asset swap, and the art gap is recorded in the scope.
- **Two hint owners** (legacy scene + player) could double-show during e12–e14. Mitigation: only the player feeds the shared manager; legacy scenes keep their own manager instance until they are migrated.
