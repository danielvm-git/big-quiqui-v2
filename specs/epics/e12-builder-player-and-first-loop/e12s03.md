## 1. Story Goal

One player builds the room from the def — background, music, `QuiQuiRig`, tray zone, hint layer, HUD,
home button, step dots — and runs the steps under a declared Fsm, so a room's rhythm is data and a step
ends on the child's effort, never on a timer.

## 2. Requirements

#### ADDED: The player builds the room and runs the steps
**After:** `ScenePlayer` composes chrome from the def (background, music, one `QuiQuiRig`, tray zone, hint layer, HUD, home, step dots) and `StepRunner` runs `LOAD → INTRO → STEP → STEP_DONE → OUTRO → EXIT` as a table-driven `Fsm`. A step ends on reported progress, never on a timer; home exits from any state with no penalty.

#### ADDED: Reactions are a closed, data-driven vocabulary
**After:** `pose · anim · sfx · voice · fx · swap · coins · wait`, executed by the player through `QuiQuiRig`, `AudioManager` and the particle presets. A template composes these; it cannot invent a new kind.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; `gdlint`/`gdformat` pass; the player scene root is a `Control` at `PRESET_FULL_RECT`.
2. **Explicit machine:** `ScenePlayer` runs `LOAD → INTRO → STEP → STEP_DONE → OUTRO → EXIT` as a table-driven `Fsm` with `legal_edges` and `max_dwell_sec`; no boolean flag clusters (CONVENTIONS §3.2, ADR-001).
3. **No step can time out or punish:** `max_dwell_sec` covers the render states only; a step has no deadline. Home exits from any state with no penalty and no confirmation.
4. **Step progress is visible without text:** N dots for N steps, re-sourced from `CareDotsBar`'s widget, filling as steps complete.
5. **Determinism:** the machine is driven by `advance(delta)` from `_process` with a `manual_step` seam, so a test steps it without waiting real time.
6. **Both aspects:** the chrome composes by canvas ratios (ADR-008) and is verified at `1920×1080` and `2048×1536` with zero letterbox bars.
7. **Observability & Traceability:** `scene.loaded`, `step.started`, `step.done`, `scene.exited` reach the diary as ids only.

## 4. Zoom-Out on the Modules This Touches

**`CareDotsBar`** — purpose: display the five care steps as dots, mirroring CareLoopManager.

- Callers: `casa.gd` (hub) only.
- Contracts to preserve: `set_completed_steps(count)` and the 0..5 clamp stay; the widget must accept a second source (step progress inside a room) without breaking the hub's use.

**`CareLoopManager`** — purpose: own the five-step care cycle and its reward, above any scene.

- Callers: `casa`, `cozinha`, `banheiro`, `closet`, `jardim`, `quarto_sono`, `celebracao`, `SceneManager` (detour guard).
- Contracts to preserve: `begin_step`, `complete_step(expected)`, `abandon_step`, `completed_steps`, `next_step`, `close_celebrate_if_needed` keep their contracts; the player reports a completed step and never transitions the loop itself.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### The player owns chrome, templates own interaction

Background, music, rig, tray, hint layer, dots and home belong to the player. A template never builds the room.

### Reactions are data, not code

A step declares pose/sfx/voice/fx/coins/wait; the player plays them through `QuiQuiRig`, `AudioManager` and the particle presets.

### QuiQui is always the same object

One `QuiQuiRig` per scene, composed from the def; templates address her through `mouth_global_center()`, `scrub_hull_global()` and `tapped`.

## 9. Risks

- **A step that can hang** is the worst regression available: a child stops touching and the room does nothing forever. Mitigation: the machine has no step deadline, and the hint layer (e12s05) is what answers silence; `max_dwell_sec` covers render states only.
- **`CareDotsBar` has two masters** (care loop and step progress) and could drift. Mitigation: one adapter, one test per source.
- **Chrome rebuilt per scene** costs frames. Mitigation: the player is the only generated scene; the rig is composed once per load, measured by the existing GUT timing (63 s baseline).
- **A reaction queue would break the same-frame promise.** Mitigation: the executor is synchronous; only `wait` defers, and it is an explicit reaction kind.
