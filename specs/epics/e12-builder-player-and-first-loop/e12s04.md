## 1. Story Goal

The first interaction primitive: drag an item to a receiver, in single, pick-one and slot modes, on top
of the existing `TouchDraggable` modes — with the reaction vocabulary that makes every valid touch answer in
the same frame and every miss gentle.

## 2. Requirements

#### ADDED: Give→Receive in three modes
**After:** `single`, `pick_one` and `slots`, implemented over `TouchDraggable`'s existing `CONSUME_ON_DROP`, `SNAP_TO_SLOT` and `FREE_PLACEMENT` modes. Every valid drop answers in the same frame; every miss returns the item with a soft sound and changes nothing.

#### ADDED: One shared template contract
**After:** A template receives its `StepDef` plus a context (rig, tray, reactions) and reports progress; it never reads the scene, never ends a step and never builds chrome.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; lint/format pass; no new file over 300 lines, no function over 20.
2. **Reuse, not rebuild:** the primitive wraps `TouchDraggable`'s `CONSUME_ON_DROP` / `SNAP_TO_SLOT` / `FREE_PLACEMENT` modes; it never adds a second drag surface (ADR-004). `touch_draggable.gd` sits at its grandfathered 326 lines and must not grow.
3. **Three modes:** `single` (one item, one receiver), `pick_one` (a tray of items, any correct one), `slots` (fixed positions, each accepting one payload).
4. **Instant answer:** every valid drop plays its sound and its QuiQui reaction in the same frame (pose plus SFX, no queue), and reward coins fly from the receiver to the HUD.
5. **Gentle miss:** a wrong or empty drop returns the item to its tray position with a soft-error SFX, changes nothing in the save and never blocks the next attempt.
6. **Mouse-echo filtering:** every handler ignores `InputEventMouse` with `device == -1`, proven under `TouchSim` and under the concurrent monkey stream.
7. **Hit targets:** every interactive area is at least `120×120 px` at both aspects.

## 4. Zoom-Out on the Modules This Touches

**`TouchDraggable`** — purpose: own the single drag surface: one finger, four modes, echo filtering, return-to-origin.

- Callers: 29 files today, including 17 scene scripts and `test_touch_draggable*`.
- Contracts to preserve: 326 lines and grandfathered by INV-09 — it may not grow by one line. `drop_target`, `snap_target`, `placement_area`, `mode`, `enabled`, the signals and the `device == -1` filter are the contract the primitive must use as-is.

**`ScrubGesture`** — purpose: own the hull test, the point count and the one loop channel for a continuous gesture.

- Callers: `touch_draggable` only.
- Contracts to preserve: `begin(host)`/`consume(point)`/`stop()`/`hull_points()` stay; no hull means no scrubs, never an error.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### Reactions are a fixed, closed set

`pose · anim · sfx · voice · fx · swap · coins · wait`. A template cannot invent a new reaction kind; it composes these.

### One finger owns one prop

The single-finger lock already enforced by `TouchDraggable` stays; the primitive never clears it out from under a drag.

### The primitive reports progress, it does not end the step

It returns progress to the runner; only the runner advances the machine.

## 8. Out of Scope

- Rub, Tap, Steer and Pan primitives (e13).
- Spawner, Pairs and Sequence modifiers (e13, e15).

## 9. Risks

- **The primitive tempts a second drag surface.** Mitigation: ADR-004 and a test that asserts exactly one `TouchDraggable` owns the finger during a drag.
- **`touch_draggable.gd` growing past its allowance** breaks INV-09. Mitigation: the primitive is a sibling that configures it, never a subclass that adds lines.
- **A miss that blocks input** (the classic stuck drag) is worse than a miss. Mitigation: the miss path is asserted to leave `enabled == true` and the item back at rest.

## Verification Script

1. `bash scripts/preflight.sh` → PASS.

4. Drag with two fingers → only one item moves and nothing fires twice.
5. `bash scripts/logs.sh tail` → exactly one `template.give` event per drop.
