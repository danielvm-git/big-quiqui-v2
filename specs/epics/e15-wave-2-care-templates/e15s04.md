## 1. Story Goal

`t38_peekaboo` as a consumer of the Tap primitive and the Spawner modifier that e13s08 already
shipped. Friends peek out, a tap giggles, and a miss changes nothing. This story does not edit
Spawner, Steer or `t50_lanes`.

## 2. Requirements

#### ADDED: t38_peekaboo
**After:** Spawner emits actors from a def set, on a def cadence, with a lifetime and a despawn, deterministic from a seed. A tap on a visible actor plays a giggle and counts once, then that actor despawns. The step completes at the def's giggle count. The template knows no room.

#### ADDED: Adaptation rule for t38_peekaboo
**After:** The design rule stands: friends peek out, a tap gives a giggle, and there are no lives. `DecrementLives`, `DecreaseLife`, `RestartScene` and `ShakeScreen` have no counterpart and no def key. An actor that hides on its own leaves quietly and does not reduce the giggle count. The same seed through `SpawnerClock` replays the same actor ids. Spawner stays the shared modifier from e13s08: `t50` is one consumer, `t38` is the next, and the e13 second-consumer stub stays.

#### REMOVED: Lives on peekaboo
**Before:** TutoTOONS `t38` shows moles and calls `DecrementLives`.
**After:** (removed) — the motion stays, the loss does not. A missed peek is quiet. The step cannot be failed.

#### ADDED: The peekaboo scene
**After:** `res://data/scenes/peekaboo.tres` is a Care play scene on the player. Its one step names `t38_peekaboo` and lists existing friend ids. The id `peekaboo` is in `SceneCatalog` and is an unlocked map destination. It is not a CareLoopManager step.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; lint/format pass; bars hold.
2. **Built from Tap + Spawner, unchanged:** the template calls the e13 Spawner (seed, cadence, set, lifetime, despawn) and Tap for the giggle. It does not add a Spawner API and it does not edit `test_spawner.gd`'s contract.
3. **A visible tap counts once:** the giggle and the sound play in the same frame; a second tap on a despawned actor does not count; a tap on an empty spot does not count.
4. **No lives:** a source scan fails on `DecrementLives`, `DecreaseLife`, `RestartScene` or `ShakeScreen`. A miss does not end the step and does not restart it.
5. **No orphans, same seed:** a run advanced by `SpawnerClock` leaves zero orphan nodes, and a second run with the same seed emits the same ids. The spawner reads no wall clock.
6. **One playable Care scene:** `peekaboo.tres` runs on the player and completes at the giggle count. Targets are at least 120 px at both aspects.

## 4. Zoom-Out on the Modules This Touches

**`Spawner` (e13s08)** — purpose: emit timed objects for any template, from a seed, and despawn them.

- Callers: `t50_lanes` now; `t38_peekaboo` in this story; `t37`, `t41` and `t46` in e16. `t26` uses it with Steer, which this story does not touch.
- Contracts to preserve: seeded determinism; no wall clock; a stopped spawner leaves no orphans; the modifier is not shaped around `t50` or around `t38`.

**`Tap` (e13s02)** — purpose: one answering touch per target, echo filtered.

- Callers: wave-1 tap templates, Sequence, Pairs, and now `t38_peekaboo`.
- Contracts to preserve: three modes; `TapButton` is the only tap surface.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### Spawner is consumed, not owned

`t38` passes a spawn table. It does not grow the modifier. The e13 stub that proves a second consumer still passes.

### A miss is quiet

No life, no shake, no restart, no sound that reads as failure. The giggle count only moves up.

### Home still leaves

The step ends on the giggle count or when the child leaves. It has no fail state.

## 9. Risks

- **Orphan nodes from peeks that never despawn.** Mitigation: despawn on tap and on lifetime, asserted with the orphan counter under `SpawnerClock`. Security for this path is `medium`, from the e13 spawner denial-of-service row. Agent C writes e15's threat model; this story does not.
- **A def key that turns lives back on.** Mitigation: the template has no such key, and the source scan fails if the life verbs appear.
- **A Spawner change "to fit peekaboo"** would break `t50` and the later consumers. Mitigation: this story only adds a caller. `SC-e15s04-P2-01` records that the Spawner script is unchanged.
