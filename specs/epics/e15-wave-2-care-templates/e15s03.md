## 1. Story Goal

The Pairs modifier, shipped through `t27_memory`: flip two cards, keep a match, turn a miss back
over, with no penalty. The Care scene is a four-card board of pictures the game already has.

## 2. Requirements

#### ADDED: Pairs modifier
**After:** Two taps compare a pair key from the def. A match stays face up. A mismatch turns both cards face down and leaves progress unchanged. Reason for Depth: matching is shared behaviour with its own states, and Tap's three modes stay closed. Pairs is the modifier the design binds to `t27`, not a fourth Tap mode and not Sequence.

#### ADDED: t27_memory
**After:** Cards flip, a seeded shuffle places them, and `foundCorrect` marks a matched pair. The board is an `Fsm` with states idle, one-up, resolving and done. Cards are pictures. The template knows no room.

#### ADDED: Adaptation rule for t27_memory
**After:** The board is 4 or 6 cards (2 or 3 pairs), which is the design rule of 4–6 cards with every card paired. A count of 5 or 7 is rejected at def load with `scene.def_rejected`. Shuffle order is a pure function of the def seed, so two runs with the same seed lay the same cards. A mismatch plays a soft sound and flips back. There is no life, no fail, and no text on a card. `Fsm.max_dwell_sec` during resolving flips the open cards down and does not complete the step.

#### ADDED: The memory scene
**After:** `res://data/scenes/memory_match.tres` is a Care play scene on the player. Its one step names `t27_memory` with four cards drawn from existing picture ids and a fixed seed. The id `memory_match` is in `SceneCatalog` and is an unlocked map destination. It is not a CareLoopManager step.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; lint/format pass; bars hold.
2. **Pairs is a modifier on Tap:** a match sticks; a mismatch returns both cards and does not change the matched set. Tap stays `any` / `count_n` / `toggle`.
3. **The board is 4 or 6 cards:** 5 and 7 fail validation. Every card id has exactly one partner.
4. **Shuffle is seeded:** the same seed and the same card list produce the same order. The shuffle reads no wall clock.
5. **A finished pair stays finished:** later taps on a matched card replay its reaction and do not break the pair. The step completes once, when every pair is found.
6. **One playable Care scene:** `memory_match.tres` runs on the player. Cards are at least 120 px at both aspects, and none of them carries readable text.

## 4. Zoom-Out on the Modules This Touches

**`Tap` (e13s02)** — purpose: answer a touch in three modes, one finger per target, echo filtered.

- Callers: the wave-1 tap templates, Sequence (e15s02), and now Pairs / `t27_memory`.
- Contracts to preserve: three modes; a spent target still answers; `TapButton` is the only tap surface; no timer inside Tap ends a step.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### The deck is data

Pair keys, card pictures and the seed live in the def. The template holds no deck of its own.

### A miss is a flip, not a loss

Both cards turn down. The matched set is unchanged. Nothing restarts the scene.

### Odd counts are rejected

A leftover card cannot be paired. Load fails before the first flip.

## 8. Out of Scope

- Sequence and `t55_find` (e15s02).
- Boards larger than 6 cards, lives, a fail state, or labels a child must read.
- Edits to `tap_button.gd`. New art, new voice files, a new `.tscn`, a new package, or a new CareLoopManager state.
- Using Spawner to deal cards. The deck is a seeded shuffle, not a spawn cadence.

## 9. Risks

- **An odd card that can never match** leaves the step with no exit. Mitigation: 5 and 7 fail at load, and the test feeds both.
- **An unseeded shuffle** makes a stuck board unreproducible. Mitigation: the order is asserted equal across two runs of the same seed.
- **Pairs becoming a Tap mode** would reopen e13s02. Mitigation: the Tap mode set is asserted unchanged.

## Verification Script

1. `bash scripts/preflight.sh` → PASS.
2. Open the `memory_match` scene on the player.
3. Flip two cards that differ → they turn down, and the step stays open.
4. Flip a matching pair → they stay up.
5. Find both pairs → the step completes. Relaunch with the same seed → the cards sit in the same places.
