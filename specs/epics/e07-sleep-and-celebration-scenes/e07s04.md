# e07s04: Full 5-step care cycle E2E — Casa → Cozinha → Banheiro → Closet → Jardim → Sono → Celebração → Casa

<!-- story: e07s04 -->
- **Epic:** `e07-sleep-and-celebration-scenes`
- **BCP:** 2
- **Playable Vertical Target:** `res://scenes/quarto_sono.tscn` + `res://scenes/celebracao.tscn`
- **Threat findings closed here:** T-E07-06 (the whole loop, once)

## 1. Story Goal
Prove the cycle a child plays: five care steps in order, then the celebration closes it, the reward is paid
once and the hub comes back usable — on both canvases.

**Scope note (honest boundary):** this test drives the two new rooms (SLEEP, and the celebration it
requests) through touch, and walks the earlier four steps through the loop's own machine. The drag/scrub
journeys of the kitchen, bathroom, closet and garden are proven by their own E2E suites
(`test_feed_e2e`, `test_bath_e2e`, `test_closet_e2e`, the garden journey). The subject here is the
**cycle**, not a re-proof of each room's gesture.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`, type gate passes.
2. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** every interactive node filters `device == -1`; one tap or drag is one action.
3. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.
1. The full journey runs with zero `fsm.illegal_transition` / `fsm.dwell_timeout` across care loop, scene manager and every room machine under `FsmProbe`.
2. Each step is credited exactly once; `completed_steps()` reaches 5; the celebration pays +5/+1 and increments `completed_cycles` once.
3. After the celebration the loop is IDLE, the Casa is on stage and its first door is available.
4. Each scene passes `GeometryOracle` with its own spec during the journey.
5. The journey runs at 1920x1080 and 2048x1536 as separate tests (singletons carry state between mounts).

## 3. Frozen Contract
`game/tests/helpers/cycle_e2e_test_case.gd` — `class_name CycleE2eTestCase`: the loader fixture,
per-scene `FsmProbe`s, `_walk_step()`, `_assert_step_credited()`, `_assert_probes_clean()`.
