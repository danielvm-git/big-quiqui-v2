## Story Goal
The Vila recycling mini-activity: a `saquinho_lixo` trash bag appears, a **ghost hand** teaches the
child to drag it to the bin (no text, no button — the TutoTOONS `DragWorldToWorldHint` +
`Utility.HintHand` pattern), and dropping it pays **+1 Coin** that *travels* from the bin to the HUD
chip while the numeral pulses. This is the first drag a child meets alone in the outdoor world, so it
is the debut of both the hint layer and the reward-juice feedback.

**Why here:** FIX-02/FIX-03 land on the recycling drag because it is the first non-cycle earn and the
first novel gesture after the care rooms — the natural "teach without words" moment.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`.
2. **Explicit State Machine:** the recycle activity (spawn -> drag -> drop -> reward -> respawn) is an
   `Fsm`; zero `illegal_transition` / `dwell_timeout` under `FsmProbe`.
3. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** every interactive node filters `device == -1`; one drag is one drop;
   the hint hand and the flying coin are `MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE`.
4. **Dual-Aspect Responsive Layout:** `GeometryOracle` passes on both canvases, hit targets >= 120px.
5. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.

### FIX-02 — hint layer (ADDED)
6. `HintManager` shows **one hint at a time** from a queue, throttles repeats with a cooldown, and
   stops cleanly; `DragHint` slides a drawn hand from a source to a target position and pulses the
   target while doing so.
7. The recycling debut enqueues a `DragHint` (hand: `saquinho_lixo` -> `lixeira`) before the first
   drag; after one successful recycle the hint is not shown again this session.
8. The hint is deterministic: with `manual_step`, `HintManager.advance(delta)` and
   `DragHint.advance(delta)` are pure functions of accumulated time, replayable in tests.

### FIX-03 — reward juice (MODIFIED)
9. `CoinTicketHud` gains `pulse_coin()` (scale pop on the coin numeral/icon, then return) and
   `fly_coin(from_global)` (spawn a coin sprite at the world position, tween it to the HUD chip, then
   pulse). Both are read-only to the economy: only `ProgressManager.add_coins(1)` changes the count.
10. On drop, the +1 Coin is paid **once** per bag through `ProgressManager.add_coins(1)`, the sparkle
    plays, and `fly_coin` + `pulse_coin` run; a second drop of the same bag pays nothing.

### Recycling activity (ADDED)
11. The bag is a `TouchDraggable` (`CONSUME_ON_DROP`), the bin is the drop target; a missed drop
    returns the bag to its start (the existing `MovableTool`/`Template47Draggable` return-to-start
    pattern).
12. Economy is conserved: coins never go negative and a double-tap on the bin never double-pays
    (same invariant family as e08s04).
