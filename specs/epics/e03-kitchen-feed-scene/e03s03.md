## 1. Story Goal
A child taps the FEED door, feeds QuiQui three foods from the tray, sees her chew and enjoy each one, and returns to the Casa with the first dot filled and BATH next.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`, and the type gate passes.
2. **Explicit State Machine:** Any stateful behavior uses `Fsm` (`res://scripts/core/fsm.gd`) with zero `fsm.illegal_transition` or `fsm.dwell_timeout` events under `FsmProbe`.
3. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** Interactive controls filter `device == -1` synthesized mouse echo and act once when the echo is present.
4. **Dual-Aspect Responsive Layout:** `GeometryOracle` passes at both `1920x1080` (16:9) and `2048x1536` (4:3 iPad) with zero letterbox bars and `>=120x120 px` hit targets.
5. **Observability & Traceability:** `bash scripts/logs.sh audit --self-test` and `bash scripts/trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.
6. **Tray:** 3 distinct foods from `FoodCatalog.pick_tray`, at least 24 px apart, all at least 220 px from the mouth at rest on both aspects, so she does not anticipate food on the tray.
7. **Wiring:** each food's `drop_target` is the rig's `mouth_zone` with `drop_margin = 120`. `drag_moved` drives `update_food_proximity`, and `drag_ended` calls `clear_food_proximity` unless the food was consumed.
8. **One bite:** on `consumed`, `READY -> CHEWING` by `transition_from`: `mastigando`, `Crumbs` burst, `nhami.ogg`. Then `PLEASURE`: `prazer`, `Hearts` burst. Then `READY` with `base`, or `FINISHED` after the third bite. Other foods are disabled outside `READY`.
9. **Care loop:** on entry, `begin_step(FEED)` is called only if `state_id()` is `IDLE`. On `FINISHED`, `complete_step(FEED)` is called only if `state_id()` is `FEED`, then `SceneManager.request_scene(CASA_PATH)`. Entering after FEED is done is free play: she still eats, no step is credited, and no `fsm.illegal_transition` is logged.
10. **Way out:** `HomeButton` calls `abandon_step()` if the loop is in `FEED` and returns to the Casa. It is ignored in `FINISHED`, so the return cannot be requested twice.
11. **Audio:** `cozinha.ogg` music and `feed_hora_de_comer.ogg` voice on entry through `AudioManager`, with graceful handling if a stream is missing. **Casa must restore `tema_principal.ogg` on arrival**, or the kitchen theme keeps playing in the hub.
12. **Idle:** after 8.0 s without a touch in `READY`, `carente` plus a `WishBubble` pulse with `cenoura.png` (DESIGN §4.3). Any drag clears it.
13. **Casa door:** the FEED door now navigates to `cozinha.tscn`; BATH, DRESS, PLAY, SLEEP and VILA stay `scene.pending`. Existing tests must stay true, updated only where they assumed the FEED scene is absent.
14. **No text:** the scene has no `Label` or text prompt.
15. **Manual run:** a windowed `godot --path game` session drags real input across the screen, because headless tests cannot prove GUI dispatch. Record the result in the verification report, including whether a real mouse double-fires on `TapButton` or `TouchDraggable`.
