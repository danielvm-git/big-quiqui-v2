## 1. Story Goal

Three movement shapes on primitives that already exist. `t37_tap_moving` taps objects the Spawner is
already moving. `t26_ride` steers freely and collects. `t41_float_pop` pops floating objects. Each one
ships with a probe def the player can run. Nothing here rebuilds Steer or the Spawner, and nothing adds
a care step.

## 2. Requirements

#### ADDED: t37_tap_moving
**After:** Moving objects come from the existing Spawner (cadence, set, lifetime, seed). A tap on one counts through the existing Tap primitive and plays a reaction in the same frame. A miss despawns with the lifetime rule. `DecreaseLife` and `ShakeScreen` have no counterpart, and no def key turns them on.

#### ADDED: t26_ride
**After:** The character follows the finger with Steer `free`, inside the bounded area the def names. Overlap with a spawned object collects it. The road has nothing that ends the step: `OnCollisionEnter2D` does not restart the scene and does not remove a life.

#### ADDED: t41_float_pop
**After:** Spawned objects float and a tap pops one. The pop is Tap plus the existing reaction vocabulary. `Slicer2DController`, `PerformSlicing` and a blade have no counterpart.

#### REMOVED: Lives, crashing and slicing
**Before:** TutoTOONS `Template37` calls `DecreaseLife` and `ShakeScreen`; `Template26` treats `OnCollisionEnter2D` as a crash; `Template41` slices launched fruit with `Slicer2DController`.
**After:** (removed) — the motion and the collect/pop stay; a life counter, a crash restart and a blade do not exist in these templates.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; lint/format pass; no file over 300 lines, no function over 20 non-comment lines.
2. **Shapes on existing primitives:** `t37` and `t41` use Tap + Spawner; `t26` uses Steer `free` + Spawner. This story does not add a gesture primitive and does not change the Spawner contract (seeded cadence, lifetime, despawn).
3. **No lives and no screen shake:** `t37` has no life counter and no shake, and a def cannot switch either on.
4. **Collect only:** `t26` collects on overlap. A collision does not crash, restart, or punish.
5. **Pop, with no blade:** `t41` pops on tap. No slicer, no swipe-cut, no blade node.
6. **Spawned nodes cannot accumulate:** every spawned object despawns on collect, pop, or lifetime. A long run leaves zero orphan nodes.
7. **No ads and no network:** the three templates contain no ad verb and no `HTTPRequest`, `HTTPClient`, `WebSocketPeer` or `OS.shell_open`.
8. **One player scene:** each template has a probe def played by `res://scenes/scene_player.tscn`. No new `.tscn`, no new art file, no new voice file, no `SaveData` field, no care-loop step.
9. **Instant answer and echo filter:** a valid tap answers with sound and a QuiQui reaction in the same frame. Handlers ignore `event.device == -1`. Hit targets are at least 120×120 px at `1920×1080` and `2048×1536`.
10. **Trace:** every new script and test carries `# story: e16s01`.

## 4. Zoom-Out on the Modules This Touches

**`Spawner` (e13s08)** — purpose: emit timed objects from a def-owned table, deterministically from a seed, and despawn them.

- Callers: `t50_lanes` today; `t37`, `t26` and `t41` in this story; `t38` and `t46` elsewhere.
- Contracts to preserve: seeded replay, no wall clock, no network; a stopped spawner leaves no orphans. This story is a consumer. It does not retune the modifier.

**`Steer` (e13s08)** — purpose: move a body with the finger, `free` inside a bound or `lanes` snapping on release.

- Callers: `t50_lanes` (`lanes`); `t26_ride` (`free`).
- Contracts to preserve: the primitive reports position. The template decides that overlap means collect.

**`Tap` (e13s02)** — purpose: one-finger press on `TapButton`, with squash, distinct-target counting, and the echo filter.

- Callers: `t21_touch_each` and the other tap templates.
- Contracts to preserve: the primitive adds no second tap surface. Moving targets are still `TapButton`s the Spawner repositions.

No new package. Godot 4.7 and GUT stay `[OK]`. Slicer2D is not ported.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### The Spawner stays shared

`t37`, `t26` and `t41` pass a spawn table. They do not own the modifier and they do not add a second spawner.

### A miss is quiet

An uncaught, unpopped object leaves when its lifetime ends. The step does not shake, restart, or count a loss.

### Despawn is the only way a spawned node ends

Collect, pop, and lifetime all free the node. The orphan counter in the standard run is the proof.

## 9. Risks

- **A life or a shake sneaking back in through a def bool.** Mitigation: `SC-e16s01-P0-01` and the source scan `SC-e16s01-P1-01`; the template has no such key.
- **Spawned nodes accumulating** until the frame budget dies. Mitigation: `SC-e16s01-P0-04` asserts zero orphans after a long run.
- **A second spawner inside `t26`.** Mitigation: the tests construct the e13 Spawner; the template files do not declare another emitter.
- **Porting Slicer2D for `t41`.** Mitigation: `SC-e16s01-P0-03` fails if a slicer or blade type appears.
