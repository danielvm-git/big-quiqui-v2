## 1. Story Goal

Three niche shapes, each playable on the player. `t42_slice_cut` follows a dotted path with the tool
already in the hand. `t46_cups` hides an object under cups and waits until the child finds it.
`t48_serve` stacks food for one guest who stays. Rub, Tap, Spawner and Give→Receive already exist; this
story adds the shapes and one probe def each.

## 2. Requirements

#### ADDED: t42_slice_cut
**After:** The def carries a polyline. The template reads points from the existing Rub / `ScrubGesture` stream and reports path coverage to the runner. The tool follows the dotted line. `Slicer2D` and a blade have no counterpart. Reason for depth: the path is template data over the gesture's points, because a new cut primitive would duplicate `ScrubGesture`.

#### ADDED: t46_cups
**After:** Cups and the hidden object come from the Spawner table and a seeded shuffle. A tap on the cup that hides the object reveals it. A tap on another cup answers and leaves the object findable. `UpdateTimer` and `TimeOut` have no counterpart, and no def key ends the step on a clock.

#### ADDED: t48_serve
**After:** One guest, one target, a stack of items from the def, delivered with the existing Give→Receive primitive (`repeat` / slots). The guest is still there after the last item. `NewClient` and `ClientExitFromScene` have no counterpart. No new economy curve and no new `SaveData` field.

#### REMOVED: Blade, timeout and a leaving guest
**Before:** TutoTOONS cuts with `Slicer2D`, ends the shell game on `TimeOut`, and cycles restaurant guests through `NewClient` / `ClientExitFromScene` with a payment.
**After:** (removed) — the path, the cups and the stack stay. A blade, a failing clock, a second guest and a new payout table do not.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** fully annotated; lint/format pass; bars hold.
2. **`t42` is Rub along a def polyline:** coverage is path progress reported to the runner. Off-path points do not complete the step. There is no blade and no new Rub mode.
3. **`t46` always leaves the object findable:** no timer ends the step. The seeded shuffle can be replayed. Wrong cups do not punish.
4. **`t48` is one guest who stays:** the stack uses Give→Receive. The guest does not exit. The template writes no save and does not read `EconomyData` to invent a price.
5. **Spawned cups cannot accumulate:** shuffle actors despawn when the reveal finishes. A repeated round leaves zero orphans.
6. **No ads and no network** in the three templates.
7. **Probe defs on the one player scene.** No new `.tscn`, art, voice, care step, or save field.
8. **Echo filter and 120 px targets** at both aspects. Multi-stage serve or shuffle order is an `Fsm` with `legal_edges` and `max_dwell_sec`, never a pair of booleans.
9. **Trace:** `# story: e16s02` on every new script and test.

- Callers: kitchen and the other drag rooms.

No new package. Godot 4.7 and GUT stay `[OK]`.

## 5. Frozen Contracts

### The runner owns completion

`t42` reports coverage. A threshold in the def decides when the step is done. The template does not complete itself on the first point.

### Cups have no clock

The step ends when the child finds the object. Waiting does not fail the round.

### One guest is the whole restaurant

The def names one client. Exit, replacement, and a payout table are absent.
