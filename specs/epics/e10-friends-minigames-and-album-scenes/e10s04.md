# e10s04: Playable Scene 17: AlbumScene (album.tscn) Sticker & Star Collection with Return-to-Origin Navigation

- **Epic:** `e10-friends-minigames-and-album-scenes`
- **BCP:** 3
- **Playable Vertical Target:** `res://scenes/album.tscn`
- **Delta:** `ADDED` (`album.tscn`, `album.gd`, `poi_table.gd`, `build_album_scene.gd`) +

## Story Goal
The payoff screen of the epic: one place where she sees everything she earned with her friends.
Three friend cards, each showing its `friend_stars` count as a row of lit stars (the stickers),
locked friends dark until the first star. The album is the vila's new door — and the door's badge
(FIX-04) lights up the moment the first star exists, because the badge component itself reads
progress; the hub gains no logic. The 300-line bar forces the POI table out of `vila.gd` on the
way in: this story must leave `vila.gd` shorter than it found it.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`.
2. **Explicit State Machine:** `AlbumScene` has no multi-stage mode to model — mount, refresh on
   `progress_changed`, navigate home; no boolean flag clusters, and any guard uses existing Fsm
   contracts (`SceneManager` fade Fsm) rather than new state.
3. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** cards and Home fire once under
   `TouchSim.tap_control(..., with_mouse_echo=true)`.
4. **Dual-Aspect Responsive Layout:** `GeometryOracle` passes at `1920x1080` and `2048x1536`, zero
   letterbox; cards and Home are `>=120x120 px`.
5. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.

### Sticker & star collection (ADDED — `res://scenes/album.tscn`)
6. One card per `FriendCatalog.FRIEND_IDS`: amigo art, and a row of five star slots
   (`star.png`) of which `stars_of(id)` are lit. A friend with zero stars renders dark
   (modulate) — present but unearned, never missing. Tapping a card replays that friend's intro
   voice line and bounces the stars (pure feedback, no navigation trap).
7. The album reflects the save at mount **and** on every `progress_changed` (a star earned while
   mounted lights up live). Stars are read through `ProgressManager.stars_of` only — a tampered
   save shows clamped values because the read path clamps (threat model); no currency, unlock or
   gate reads stars anywhere in the game.
8. `HomeButton` returns to the vila (return-to-origin: the album is entered only from the vila in
   this story — minigames return to the festa, the album to the vila; recorded in ADR-012) and
   closes a dangling `CELEBRATE`.

### The vila door + badge (MODIFIED)
9. `door_badge.gd` gains an injectable rule: `@export var rule: String = "placed"`
   (`"placed"` keeps today's furniture behaviour byte-for-byte) and a new `"stars"` rule comparing
   `total_stars()` (sum over `FriendCatalog.FRIEND_IDS`) against `@export var required_stars: int`.
   The component still reads `ProgressManager` itself and still swaps art only on change.
10. The vila gains exactly one POI, `ALBUM -> res://scenes/album.tscn`, with a stars-rule
    `DoorBadge` on the door (locked art until the first star). The POI constants and scene map
    move to a new `game/scripts/core/poi_table.gd`; `vila.gd` keeps a thin alias so existing
    callers and tests do not churn. **`vila.gd` ends this story at fewer than its current 299
    lines** (the bar is paid down, never waived — the e09s02 precedent).

## Test Scenarios
- `SC-E10-P0-20` the album mounts from an empty save: three dark cards, zero lit stars, both
  aspects clean, GeometryOracle + hit-target floors pass
- `SC-E10-P0-21` stars earned before mount light the right card; a `progress_changed` while
  mounted refreshes live; a tampered out-of-range save shows clamped (0..5) values
- `SC-E10-P0-22` Home returns to the vila; the vila `ALBUM` door navigates to the album
- `SC-E10-P1-23` the stars-rule badge: locked at 0 total stars, unlocked at 1, art swaps once (no
  thrash on unrelated progress changes); the placed-rule party badge behaviour is unchanged
- `SC-E10-P1-24` `vila.gd` is shorter than 299 lines and every existing vila test still passes
  (the extraction is behaviour-neutral)

## Risks
- **`vila.gd` is at 299/300 lines** — the POI extraction must land in the same story as the ALBUM
  entry, or the bar is breached. The alias keeps `VilaScene.POI_SCENES` reads working.
- `door_badge.gd` is shared with the live party badge: the default rule must keep the e09s03
  tests green without edits (additive-only change, pinned by SC-E10-P1-23).
- The album must never become a navigation hub this story — minigame jumps from reward screens
  are explicitly out of scope (recorded in ADR-012 as a future nicety, not a gap).
