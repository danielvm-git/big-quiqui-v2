## Story Goal
The continuous-gesture minigame: Pinguim stands on an ice lane, she drags him left and right, and
falling balls and bubbles pop into her score when they reach him. One timed round, everything
counted at the end, coins per catch and a star for a full net. The whole round runs inside the
pure `advance(delta)` step (the `manual_step` seam), so headless tests replay it exactly — no
real-time flakiness, ever.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`.
2. **Explicit State Machine:** `PenguinScene` uses a table-driven `Fsm`
   (`INTRO -> RUNNING -> RESULT -> REWARD`); the penguin drag uses the shared `TouchDraggable`
   internal Fsm in `CONTINUOUS_SCRUB` mode over the lane; zero `illegal_transition` /
   `dwell_timeout` under `FsmProbe`.
3. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** one drag from `TouchSim.drag_events(..., with_mouse_echo=true)`
   moves the penguin once per event; the echo never doubles the gesture.
4. **Dual-Aspect Responsive Layout:** `GeometryOracle` passes at `1920x1080` and `2048x1536`, zero
   letterbox; the lane (the drag surface) and Home are `>=120x120 px` wide/high.
5. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.

### The catch round (ADDED)
6. The penguin is a `TouchDraggable` `CONTINUOUS_SCRUB` prop whose `scrub_target` is the lane: the
   `scrubbed(global_point)` callback sets his x from the finger, clamped to the lane, y fixed —
   one verb, no mode switches mid-round.
7. `CatchItem`s spawn from a schedule driven by `advance` (interval and fall speed from
   `EconomyData`-style tuning: `catch_spawn_sec = 1.1`, `catch_fall_px_sec = 260`, floored > 0),
   alternate `bola.png` / `bolha.png`, fall top→bottom, and are removed at the floor. A catch is
   a rect intersection between an item and the penguin's catch zone during `advance`; a caught
   item pops (`bolha.ogg`) and disappears — never a second catch of the same item (idempotent per
   item, the double-count threat).
8. `RUNNING` lasts `catch_round_sec = 20` of accumulated `advance` time (not wall clock), then
   `RESULT`: coins = one per catch (paid once, atomically, at settle), star if
   `catch_star_target = 6` catches or more (`add_star("pinguim")` refuses at cap, always safe to
   call). Below the target she still keeps her coins — effort pays, the star is mastery.
   `RESULT -> REWARD` shows the take; a second settle pays nothing.
9. `seed_spawns(types: Array[String])` pins the spawn schedule for tests; `catch_count() -> int`
   is the read side. Missed items cost nothing and end nothing — no fail state for ages 4–7.

### PenguinScene (ADDED — `res://scenes/pinguim_jogo.tscn`)
10. `bg_vila.jpg` background (no penguin-specific bg exists; the ice lane is a builder-drawn
    `ColorRect` band per the ADR-010 stopgap precedent), `QuiQuiRig`, `CoinTicketHud`,
    `HomeButton`, replay. `penguin_intro.ogg` on mount; missing art/audio log `audio.missing_art`.
11. Replay restarts `RUNNING` with a fresh schedule; `HomeButton` returns to `festa.tscn`
    (return-to-origin) at any state — a running round is simply abandoned (no pay, no wedge).

## Test Scenarios
- `SC-E10-P0-14` a spawned item that reaches the catch zone with the penguin under it counts
  exactly once and pops; the same item cannot count twice
- `SC-E10-P0-15` an item reaching the floor is removed and counts nothing; the round ends at
  `catch_round_sec` of accumulated advance time, not wall clock
- `SC-E10-P0-16` `RESULT` settle pays coins == catch count exactly once; star granted at ≥ target,
  refused at cap without error; below target still pays coins
- `SC-E10-P0-17` one mouse-echo drag moves the penguin once; penguin x clamps to the lane on both
  aspects; GeometryOracle clean at 1920×1080 and 2048×1536
- `SC-E10-P1-18` Home mid-round abandons cleanly (no pay, no wedge, festa restored); replay runs a
  fresh schedule
- `SC-E10-P1-19` FsmProbe clean across a full seeded round driven entirely by `advance` — the
  zero-real-time proof

## Risks
- **Real-time flakiness is the classic trap here** — everything (spawn, fall, clock, catch) must
  live in `advance(delta)` with `manual_step`; the integration test never awaits engine frames to
  progress the round.
- Spawn/fall tuning are data with floors (a hand-edited `0` must not freeze or flood the round:
  interval floored at a sane minimum, tested).
- SFX per catch could stack: pops go through `AudioManager`'s pooled `play_sfx` (existing
  contract), never per-item players.
