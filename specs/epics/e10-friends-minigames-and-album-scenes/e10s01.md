## Story Goal
The first **mint by design**: she plays with a friend and earns coins and a star. At the party she
taps Pato, the duck hops onto one of three dance pads, and she taps the pad he landed on — three
good answers win the round. No fail state (a miss just replays the pose), no reading, no timer
pressure. This story also lays the shared rails every e10 minigame reuses: the friend catalog
(ids → art/scene/voice paths), the reward table in `EconomyData`, the star mutators in
`ProgressManager`, and the friend-tap entry at the party.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`.
2. **Explicit State Machine:** `DuckDanceScene` uses a table-driven `Fsm`
   (`INTRO -> SHOWING -> WAITING -> REWARD`); zero `illegal_transition` / `dwell_timeout` under
   `FsmProbe`.
3. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** pads fire once under `TouchSim.tap_control(..., with_mouse_echo=true)`
   (the synthesized echo never doubles the answer).
4. **Dual-Aspect Responsive Layout:** `GeometryOracle` passes at `1920x1080` and `2048x1536`, zero
   letterbox, `>=120x120 px` hit targets (pads, Home, replay).
5. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.

### Friend rails (ADDED — shared by e10s02..e10s04)
6. `FriendCatalog` owns the friend table: ids `pato`, `urso`, `pinguim` (validated by `Ids`), each
   mapping to amigo art, its minigame scene path and its intro voice line. No other file hardcodes
   these paths; `festa.gd`'s inline `FRIEND_IDS` moves here.
7. `EconomyData.minigame_coins: Dictionary` pays one coin bundle per win, floored at `0`, default
   `{"pato": 3, "urso": 3, "pinguim": 3}` — tuning is data (FIX-05), never a scene constant.
8. `ProgressManager.add_star(friend_id) -> bool` increments `friend_stars[id]` by one up to
   `SaveData.MAX_STARS = 5`, validates the id against `FriendCatalog.FRIEND_IDS`, writes once
   (atomic) and emits `progress_changed`; at the cap it refuses (`false`) and mutates nothing.
   `stars_of(friend_id) -> int` is the read side (0 for unknown ids). No `SaveData` schema change:
   `friend_stars` and `_clean_stars` already round-trip.

### DuckDanceScene (ADDED — `res://scenes/pato_danca.tscn`)
9. Three rounds. Each round: the duck hops onto one of three pads (left/centre/right — the pose,
   because no pose art exists; pads are builder-drawn `ColorRect`s per the ADR-010 ColorRect-hand
   precedent), then the pads pulse and accept taps. A correct tap plays `success.ogg` and advances
   the round; a wrong tap plays `soft_error.ogg` and the duck **repeats the same pose** — no
   punishment, no lost round, no fail state for ages 4–7.
10. After the third correct answer the scene enters `REWARD` once: pays
    `EconomyData.minigame_coins["pato"]` coins, calls `add_star("pato")`, pops confetti, and the
    HUD coin flies (`CoinTicketHud.fly_coin`, FIX-03 juice). A second settle in `REWARD` pays
    nothing (idempotent — the echo/double-tap threat from the threat model).
11. A replay button (`REWARD -> INTRO`) restarts the three rounds; `HomeButton` always navigates
    back to `festa.tscn` (return-to-origin) and closes a dangling `CELEBRATE`.
12. `duck_intro.ogg` plays on mount; missing art/audio log `audio.missing_art` and degrade
    gracefully (never crash, never lock input).
13. `festa.gd`: each `Friend_*` node is a tappable (`TapButton`, group `hit_target`) that requests
    `FriendCatalog.scene_path(id)` — but only while the party is `DANCING` (friends are hidden
    while locked, so a locked party exposes no minigame).

## Test Scenarios
- `SC-E10-P0-01` `add_star` climbs 0→5 and refuses the sixth without mutating; bad ids refused;
  `stars_of` reads back; the save round-trips (reload keeps stars)
- `SC-E10-P0-02` a win pays coins + star exactly once (double settle / echo tap pays nothing twice)
- `SC-E10-P0-03` three correct answers reach `REWARD`; a wrong answer replays the same pose and
  the round counter does not advance (FsmProbe clean throughout)
- `SC-E10-P0-04` the scene boots clean on both aspects with an empty save; pads/Home/replay are
  `>=120 px`; mouse-echo taps fire once
- `SC-E10-P0-05` replay restarts three rounds from `REWARD`; Home returns to `festa.tscn`
- `SC-E10-P1-06` the party's friend taps navigate to the friend's minigame while `DANCING`, and
  a locked party shows no friends (no minigame entry)
- `SC-E10-P1-07` `FriendCatalog` rejects non-friend/unsafe ids and maps every path that exists on
  disk (art, scene, voice)

## Risks
- **Reward duplication is the epic's top threat** (threat model): payout lives only in the
  `REWARD` settle guarded by the Fsm, and the idempotence test is P0.
- `festa.gd` grows (friend taps) — it starts at 215 lines; the budget to 300 is enough, but the
  friend-loop must reuse the existing per-friend helpers, not clone them.
- Pose = pad position is a scope-honest reading of "pose-matching" with zero pose art; recorded in
  ADR-012 so a reviewer reads it as decided, not missed.
