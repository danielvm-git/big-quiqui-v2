## Story Goal
The magnetic jigsaw: the bear's portrait is sliced at runtime into four quadrant pieces
(`AtlasTexture` over `bear.png` — no new art), shuffled into a tray, and she drags each piece onto
its matching ghost slot. A drop near the right slot snaps in and locks; a drop elsewhere flies
home, no scolding. Four locked pieces win coins and a star for Urso. This is the drag-verb
minigame: every drag goes through the shared `TouchDraggable` in `SNAP_TO_SLOT` mode, and the
grandfathered `touch_draggable.gd` (331 lines) must not grow by a single line.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`.
2. **Explicit State Machine:** `BearPuzzleScene` uses a table-driven `Fsm`
   (`INTRO -> PLAYING -> REWARD`); the piece drag uses the shared `TouchDraggable` internal Fsm;
   zero `illegal_transition` / `dwell_timeout` under `FsmProbe` on both.
3. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** one drag from `TouchSim.drag_events(..., with_mouse_echo=true)`
   moves a piece exactly once; the echo never double-snaps.
4. **Dual-Aspect Responsive Layout:** `GeometryOracle` passes at `1920x1080` and `2048x1536`, zero
   letterbox; every piece, Home and replay is `>=120x120 px` (2×2 quadrants of the board keep
   pieces far above the floor).
5. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.

### Puzzle pieces (ADDED — `puzzle_piece.gd`)
6. A `PuzzlePiece` wraps a `TouchDraggable` in `SNAP_TO_SLOT` (the `FurnitureProp` pattern): it
   knows its piece id (`0..3`), its `AtlasTexture` quadrant of `bear.png`, and its slot. The tray
   shows the four pieces shuffled; the board shows four darkened ghost copies of the same
   quadrants (the slot art is the answer key she can see — pre-reader friendly).
7. `lock_in()` disables the piece (`enabled = false`) and settles it centred in its slot; a locked
   piece never accepts a new drag (the double-lock threat from the threat model is a no-op, not a
   duplicate). The piece stays visually in the slot after the snap; the underlying fly-home is
   neutralized by the lock, and the observable contract (snapped → parked in slot, inert) is what
   the tests pin.
8. A drop inside a **wrong** slot's margin does not lock: the piece flies home and emits nothing
   (SNAP_TO_SLOT only snaps to its own `snap_target`; the scene wires one target per piece).

### BearPuzzleScene (ADDED — `res://scenes/urso_puzzle.tscn`)
9. Board (`bg_puzzle.jpg` background), tray, `QuiQuiRig`, `CoinTicketHud`, `HomeButton`,
   `HintLayer`/`HintManager` — the e08 hint component teaches the very first drag (FIX-02, the
   parity backlog's promised reuse).
10. All four pieces locked → `REWARD` once: pays `minigame_coins["urso"]`, `add_star("urso")`,
    confetti + `success.ogg` + HUD coin fly. A second settle pays nothing (idempotent).
11. Replay (`REWARD -> INTRO`) reshuffles and re-deals four unlocked pieces; `HomeButton` returns
    to `festa.tscn` (return-to-origin) and closes a dangling `CELEBRATE`. Home is live mid-drag
    never wedged: a drag in flight aborts cleanly on scene exit.
12. `bear_intro.ogg` plays on mount; missing art/audio log `audio.missing_art` and degrade.

## Test Scenarios
- `SC-E10-P0-08` a drag onto the right slot locks the piece (`snapped` → parked, `is_locked`,
  inert to a second drag); locked count reaches 4 through four real `TouchSim` drags
- `SC-E10-P0-09` a drag onto the wrong slot or the floor flies home, emits nothing, locks nothing
- `SC-E10-P0-10` the fourth lock settles `REWARD` exactly once: coins + star paid once, echo-proof
- `SC-E10-P0-11` both aspects clean with an empty save; pieces/tray/Home `>=120 px`; one mouse-echo
  drag moves one piece once
- `SC-E10-P1-12` replay from `REWARD` re-deals four unlocked pieces; Home returns to `festa.tscn`
- `SC-E10-P1-13` the tray is a permutation of the four quadrants (seeded) and the slot art matches
  the piece art (the answer key is visible)

## Risks
- **`touch_draggable.gd` is grandfathered (331 lines): this story must not touch it.** All
  lock-in behaviour lives in `puzzle_piece.gd`/`urso_puzzle.gd`. If SNAP_TO_SLOT genuinely cannot
  express the park-in-slot observable, the fix is a scene-level settle, not a core edit.
- The fly-home vs park-in-slot race: the lock must be idempotent and final; the test pins the
  observable (piece rests in slot, cannot drag) rather than the tween internals.
- `bear.png` quadrant regions are pixel maths on the loaded texture size; a wrong aspect could
  mis-slice — the seeded test asserts each piece's region covers an exact quarter.
