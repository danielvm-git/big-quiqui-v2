## 1. Story Goal
Prove the bath the way a child plays it: every gesture delivered as touch through `TouchSim`, from the
Casa door to the Casa door, on all three canvases, with the care loop, the scene manager and the diary
watched by `FsmProbe`. Then prove the thing this epic exists for: on 4:3, every foam patch stays on her
body, before and after a resize, and a resize never resurrects a patch the child already cleaned.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`.
2. **Explicit State Machine:** the journey runs with zero `fsm.illegal_transition` / `fsm.dwell_timeout`
   across three machines (care loop, scene manager, bath stage) under `FsmProbe`.
3. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** every gesture in the journey is a `TouchSim.drag_events` /
   `tap_events` sequence with the mouse echo present; one press is one gesture.
4. **Dual-Aspect Responsive Layout:** `GeometryOracle` with `STEP_ROOM_SPEC` passes on the canvas the
   journey runs on; patches are asserted on `1920x1080`, `1920x1440` and `2048x1536`.
5. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.
6. **The journey:** bath door → bathroom → soak 8 → rinse → dry → `brilhando`, BATH credited once,
   `completed_steps() == 2`, Casa requested exactly once, back at the hub.
7. **Containment before and after resize:** every patch centre inside the hull at `1920x1440`, and again
   after the canvas becomes `2048x1536` (T-E04-07).
8. **No resurrection:** a resize after two patches are soaked leaves exactly two soaked and keeps the
   cleaned ones invisible.
9. **Leaving is not earning:** the HomeButton mid-stage abandons the step, credits nothing, keeps the
   device at `REST`, and asks for the Casa once.

## 3. Frozen Contract
`game/tests/helpers/bath_e2e_test_case.gd` — `class_name BathE2eTestCase`: the viewport/loader fixture,
`_advance_loop_to_bath()`, `_open_casa(size)`, `_enter_bath()`, `_scrub_patch(i)`, `_sweep(device, steps)`
(three alternating passes, because one pass starts on the device), `_rinse()`, `_dry()`,
`_assert_oracle_clean(size)`, `_assert_patches_on_the_body(size)`, `_assert_probes_clean()`.
