# e04s03: BathScene Stages 2 & 3 (RINSE Showerhead with gota Stream & DRY Towel -> quiqui_brilhando)

## 1. Story Goal
The bath is playable end to end: rinse her with the showerhead, dry her with the towel, and she comes out
shiny. BATH is credited exactly once, and leaving mid-stage abandons the step instead of crediting it.
Stages advance on **effort** (scrub points inside the hull), never on a timer, so a child who stops
rubbing is not carried forward and a child who rubs the wallpaper gets nothing.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria
1. **Strict Typing & Lint:** `gdlint game/ && gdformat --check game/` exits `0`, type gate passes.
2. **Explicit State Machine:** `SOAP -> SOAP_DONE -> RINSE -> DRY -> FINISHED` on one `Fsm` with the
   declared edges only, zero `fsm.illegal_transition` / `fsm.dwell_timeout` under `FsmProbe`.
3. **Touch & Mouse-Echo Safety:** all three devices are `CONTINUOUS_SCRUB` targets; the echo filter and
   the single-finger latch are unchanged.
4. **Dual-Aspect Responsive Layout:** `GeometryOracle` passes with `STEP_ROOM_SPEC` at `1920x1080` and
   `2048x1536`; the edge-parked devices stay inside the 36 px inset on both.
5. **Observability & Traceability:** `logs.sh audit --self-test` and `trace-stories.sh --strict` exit `0`.
6. **Rinse is effort:** only scrub points inside the hull count; `RINSE_POINTS` of them end the stage;
   points on the wallpaper count nothing.
7. **Devices hand over:** the sponge is disabled when RINSE starts, the showerhead when DRY starts, the
   towel when FINISHED arrives — one device in hand at a time, the others invisible.
8. **Shiny finish:** the last dry pass sets `brilhando` and the room requests the Casa once.
9. **BATH credited once (T-E04-02):** `complete_step(BATH)` happens only when the loop is actually in
   BATH, is guarded by `credited`, and a second `complete_step(BATH)` is refused by the loop itself.
10. **Free play after the step:** replaying the bath when the loop is past BATH finishes the room and
    credits nothing.
11. **Abandon mid-stage (T-E04-06):** the HomeButton aborts every device (owner released, loop stopped),
    abandons the step when it is uncredited, and leaves the loop IDLE.

## 3. Frozen Contract
`BathScene` gains:

```gdscript
const RINSE_POINTS: int = 10
const DRY_POINTS: int = 8
const STAGE_GAP_SEC: float = 0.8
var rinsed: int = 0

```

`banheiro.tscn` gains `Shower` and `Towel` (`TouchDraggable`, `CONTINUOUS_SCRUB`, group `hit_target`),
parked centre-bottom and right-bottom inside the inset, hidden and disabled until their stage.

- `SC-E04-P0-20` the machine walks SOAP → RINSE → DRY → FINISHED on effort, with one device in hand
- `SC-E04-P0-21` BATH is credited exactly once, and a replay credits nothing
