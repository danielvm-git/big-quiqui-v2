## Summary

The gates are green and most of the epic holds up when tested through the player: pads and map, rub chain, lanes, reward, prize, the loader grammar, and the coverage gate. The problem is one seam that nothing tests. Every tap test sends the event straight to the control's `_gui_input` (`PlayerTestCase.send`). The "reachable" gate only checks that a rect lies on screen. Nobody asks which control a finger actually lands on.

In the real `scene_player.tscn`, `QuiQuiRig` is a later sibling than `TrayZone` and its `mouse_filter` is `STOP`. Its rect is about 294 px wide (813..1107 at 1920x1080) and 620 px tall, in the middle of the tray. The templates that put `TapButton`s in the tray (t21, t51, t45) place them at x = 650 + 160 * index. So the second and later targets sit under QuiQui and cannot be touched. The pad templates (t25, t50) and t0_title found this problem and quiet the rig. The tray templates did not.

Result: t51's second prize can never be picked, t21 targets after the first never answer, and t45's only scarf in `dress_probe` is unreachable at 4:3 and a 19 px sliver at 16:9. The loader also has a few gaps that let a def load and then play badly (permanent locks, an unfinishable `toggle`, an unloadable mixed dress-and-decorate def).

Findings: 2 must-fix, 5 should-fix, 6 consider. Verdict: **FAIL**.

## Findings

### A1 (must-fix): t21 and t51 targets after the first are under QuiQui's hit rect and cannot be touched

- Evidence:
  - `game/scenes/scene_player.tscn:30-40` declares `TrayZone` (anchors 0.5/1.0, offsets -310..310 by -466..154). So the tray spans x 650..1270 and y 614..1234 at 1920x1080. `:51-58` declares `QuiQuiRig` after it, so it is hit-tested first.
  - `game/scripts/components/quiqui_rig.gd:67` sets `mouse_filter = STOP` and has no `_has_point` override. `_refit` shrinks the rig to `size.y * opaque_aspect`. For `quiqui_base.png` the opaque box is 412x868, which gives 294 px wide, so the rig covers x 813..1107 and y 222..842 at 16:9.
  - At 4:3 (viewport 1920x1440 under `expand`) the tray top is y 974 and the rig covers x 763..1157, y 296..1123.
  - `game/scripts/templates/t21_touch_each.gd:78-79` and `game/scripts/templates/t51_prize_pick.gd:80` place target `i` at tray-local `(160 * i, 0)`, so global x 650 + 160 * i, y 614..734.
  - Target 0 (650..770) is free. Target 1 (810..930) is covered by 117 of its 120 px at 16:9, and fully covered at 4:3. Target 2 (970..1090) is fully covered.
  - `TapButton` takes input in `_gui_input` (`tap_button.gd:48`). A press on a covered target goes to the rig, which is `STOP`, and the button never sees it.
  - The shipped probe defs hit this. `game/data/scenes/prize_probe.tres` lists `plush`, `pinwheel`, and `touch_each_probe.tres` lists `apple`, `grape`. The second item is dead.
- Why the tests are green:
  - `test_surfaces_player_e2e.gd` (`test_touching_each_object_through_the_player...` and `test_one_prize_tap_saves_and_the_other_does_nothing`) call `send(buttons[n], ...)` straight into the button's `_gui_input`. That skips GUI dispatch and z-order. The prize test even taps `buttons[1]` and asserts "no second pick", which a real finger could never do.
  - `test_template_aspects.gd:33` calls `_unreachable`, which only checks `view.encloses(rect)` (see A3).
  - The same team found this problem for the pads: `test_map_player_e2e.gd` (`test_the_rig_does_not_cover_the_map...`) and `test_t50_lanes_e2e.gd` (`test_the_rig_does_not_cover_the_middle_lane`) assert `quiet_rig`. Nothing guards the tray rows.
- Acceptance criteria broken:
  - e13s02 AC4 ("every object answers") and Verification Script step 3 ("tap each object").
  - e13s09 AC2 ("a set of prizes from the def, one tap to pick"). The child can only ever take the first prize.
  - This is a defect that reaches the child, not a lint bar.
- Fix:
  - Option 1: put the tap row where no sibling above the tray covers it (left of the rig, or lay the row out outside x 813..1107).
  - Option 2: give the tray templates the same treatment as the pads, with `TemplateKit.quiet_rig(ctx)` on setup and `restore_rig` on teardown. t0_title already does this for the whole host.
  - Either way, add the hit-test guard from A3 so this cannot come back.

### A2 (must-fix): t45's dress strip is under QuiQui, so `dress_probe` cannot be played by a finger

- Evidence:
  - `game/scripts/templates/t45_dress_decorate.gd:185-193` puts a 452x160 `ScrollContainer` at tray (0,0). That is x 650..1102, y 614..774.
  - `_fill_row` adds the category tabs first and then the items in an `HBoxContainer` with 24 px separation. For `dress_probe.tres` (one item, one category) the row is: tab `warm` at x 650..770, `blue_scarf` at x 794..914.
  - The row is 264 px wide, narrower than the strip (452), so nothing scrolls and the scarf cannot be brought clear of the rig.
  - The rig covers x 813..1107 at 16:9. The scarf has a 19 px sliver (794..812) outside it. At 4:3 the rig covers 763..1157, so the scarf is fully covered.
  - The tab is partly under the rig at 4:3 (7 px overlap) and free at 16:9.
  - The rest of the strip, including the scroll surface, is under the rig for the same reason as A1. The bin (tray x 476, so global 1126..1246) is free at 16:9 and 31 px overlapped at 4:3.
- Test seam: `test_surfaces_player_e2e.gd` (`test_the_dress_strip_snaps_and_the_bin_clears`) sends the touch to the scarf's `_gui_input` directly.
- Acceptance criteria broken: e13s05 AC2 (the strip's items snap onto slots, the look is saved). Today a child cannot dress QuiQui from the only def that uses t45.
- Fix: same as A1. Add a real hit-test to the guard so this one is also caught. Re-lay the strip so the first screenful of items is outside the rig rect at both canvases.

### A3 (should-fix): the "reachable at every aspect" gate cannot fail on occlusion and is satisfied by Home alone

- Evidence:
  - `game/tests/integration/test_template_aspects.gd:27` asserts `_targets(player).size() > 0`. The target set is the `hit_target` group, and `HomeButton` is in that group (`scene_player.tscn`, group `hit_target`). So the assertion is true for every def, including defs whose template added no target.
  - `_targets` only finds `TapButton`s from `TemplateKit.tap_button`, map pins and t20 items. `RubSurface._build_tool` (`rub_surface.gd`, tool creation) and t50's basket never join `hit_target`. So `bathroom_probe`, `rub_reveal_probe` and `penguin_probe` pass with only Home checked, and the 120 px floor is never read from the rub tool at the other aspects.
  - `_unreachable` (`:50-56`) is `view.encloses(rect)` plus "inside a ScrollContainer" plus "map pin that `follow` can reach". It has no notion of the control above the target. That is how A1 and A2 passed.
  - The self-check `test_a_target_that_no_finger_can_reach_fails_the_check` (`:38`) only proves that a target at (9000, 9000) fails.
- Fix:
  - Add a helper that finds the top-most mouse-stopping control at a point, walking the player's tree in reverse order and honouring `mouse_filter` and `visible`. Assert that it is the target, one of its ancestors, or one of its descendants.
  - Add the rub tool and the lane basket to `hit_target`, and assert that each def exposes at least one non-Home target.
  - Add a self-check with an overlapping `STOP` sibling.

### A4 (should-fix): the loader accepts a lock that can never open

- Evidence:
  - `game/scripts/core/step_params.gd:97-99` only checks `destination_locks` against `LOCK_PATTERN` (`^[A-Z][A-Z_]{0,23}$`). `MapDef.is_locked` (`map_def.gd`, `is_locked`) opens a door only when `open_steps.has(rule)`, and `open_steps` is built from `CareSteps.ORDER` = FEED, BATH, DRESS, PLAY, SLEEP.
  - A typo (`"FEEED"`) or `"CELEBRATE"` or `"IDLE"` loads cleanly and the door stays dimmed forever. `MapDef.next_care` then points at whatever care step is next, which never unlocks that door.
  - The threat model row "a step with no exit is introduced by a new template" is about exactly this kind of door. A permanent lock never ends and shows no error.
- Fix: in `_clean_optional`, require each non-empty lock to be in `CareSteps.ORDER`. Add a `test_step_params` case for `"FEEED"` and `"CELEBRATE"` and reject with a reason id.

### A5 (should-fix): a def that mixes wearable items and free-placed furniture cannot load, and the unit test builds exactly that def

- Evidence:
  - `StepParams._shared` (`step_params.gd:74-77`) runs `_clean_ids(step.slot_ids, false)`. That calls `Ids.is_valid` on every entry, and `Ids.is_valid("")` is false (`ids.gd`: `value.is_empty()` returns false). So a `slot_ids` entry of `""` returns `bad_id`.
  - The template depends on `""` meaning free place (`t45_dress_decorate.gd:85-87` `choose` and `:104-110` `place_free`, and `_slot_for` returning `""`). The unit test `test_t45_dress_decorate.gd` (`_mount_with_tray`, `step.slot_ids = ["neck", "head", ""]`) mounts that shape straight through `TemplateCatalog.instantiate`, and the test `test_a_tap_on_a_furniture_piece_places_it_free` claims "free place is reachable by a tap".
  - Through `SceneDefLoader` that same def is rejected with `scene.def_rejected / bad_id`. A real def can have all slots set (all wearable) or none set (all furniture), never both.
  - The spec text (e13s05 AC2) says "items snap onto slots or free place" in one strip.
- Fix: allow an empty entry in `slot_ids` (move it to the optional check). Add a loader-level test with the mixed def. Add the real def or a probe that uses both.

### A6 (should-fix): the number of tray targets is unbounded and rows run off screen

- Evidence:
  - `step_params.gd` has caps for lanes, cadence, lifetime and count, but nothing caps `item_ids` for t21, t51 or t45.
  - t21 and t51 place target `i` at global x 650 + 160 * i. The target for `i = 8` starts at x 1930, past the 1920 canvas edge.
  - In t21 `toggle` mode the step is done only when every target is on (`TapPrimitive.progress`, `targets = _buttons.size()`), so a nine-item `toggle` def loads and can never finish. Home is the only exit. In `any` and `count_n` the first targets still work.
- Fix: cap `item_ids` for the tray-row templates in `StepParams` (and wrap or scroll the row so the cap is the true limit), and add a loader test for the cap. A later migration (roda prizes, playroom objects) is the first place this would bite.

### A7 (should-fix): `t32_reward` models three states with two booleans

- Evidence: `game/scripts/templates/t32_reward.gd:8-9` has `var _star: bool` and `var _continued: bool`. The valid combinations are (false,false) unlit, (true,false) lit, (true,true) carried on. `collect()` (`:43`) flips both. This is a state machine in a flag pair, which CONVENTIONS section 3.2 and AGENTS.md forbid ("NEVER encode multi-step modes with 2+ boolean flags"). t51 has the same shape with `_picked` (string) plus `_continued` (bool), and is the lesser case.
- Fix: model it with `Fsm` (`UNLIT`, `LIT`, `DONE`) or one enum-like state string, with `legal_edges`. Keep the template's public seam (`star_lit`, `progress`).

### A8 (consider): t45 gives a silent, dead tap for a piece that is not wearable or not owned

- Evidence: `t45_dress_decorate.gd` `snap` (`:89`) returns false when `ProgressManager.wear` refuses, and `place` returns false when `place_furniture` refuses (furniture not owned). Neither plays a sound, a pose or a dim. A child touching a locked or unknown piece gets nothing back. The frozen contract in e13s02 ("a dead control teaches a child that touching does not work") is stated for t21 only, but the same child-facing rule applies.
- Fix: call `TemplateKit.play_miss(_ctx)` on a refused snap or place.

### A9 (consider): a def can name a reserved player node id as a rub layer

- Evidence: `RubSurface._existing` (`rub_surface.gd:142`) resolves a layer id through `PlayerNodeTable`, which has `home`, `tray` and `quiqui_mouth` registered by `ScenePlayer._register_nodes` (`scene_player.gd:241`). A t34 or t23 def with `item_ids = ["home"]` or `["tray"]` is valid for `StepParams` and fades the Home button or the whole tray (and so the rub tool) as a "layer". `teardown` restores the alpha.
- Fix: reject the reserved ids in `StepParams` for the rub templates, or only claim a node that carries the `KEPT` meta.

### A10 (consider): t51 re-entry with a saved prize that is not in this def's list leaves dead buttons

- Evidence: `t51_prize_pick.gd:14-16` only adopts the saved prize when `_prizes().has(saved)`. Otherwise `_picked` stays empty, a tap calls `ProgressManager.pick_prize`, `PrizeLedger.pick` refuses (the cycle already has a pick), `pick` returns false, and the step can never finish. Home is the exit. This needs two prize defs in one cycle, so it is not on any shipped path today.
- Fix: when the ledger already holds a pick, treat the step as picked (show the saved id lit or, if it is not in the list, let one tap carry on).

### A11 (consider): the two new persisted fields have no reload test and no version note

- Evidence: `save_data.gd` adds `picked_prize` and `prize_cycle` to `to_dictionary` and `from_dictionary` (and clamps them), which is sound. `SaveData.version` stays 1, which matches how `owned_furniture` and `friend_stars` were added earlier (additive, defaulted on read). But no test writes a pick, reloads from disk, and reads it back (`picked_prize` appears only in t51 and the surfaces e2e, both in-memory). The AGENTS.md rule says to bump the version on schema change.
- Fix: add one save round-trip test for the two fields, and write one line in the ADR-005 note that additive fields keep version 1.

### A12 (consider): the e13 templates never report input to the player, so a hint can replay over a child who is already playing

- Evidence: `ScenePlayer._note_input` (`scene_player.gd:225`) is only wired to the rig tap and Home (`_wire_buttons`). `TapButton` taps in the tray, `TouchPad` presses, rub drags and lane steering never call it. `HintLayer.advance` shows the hint when `idle_sec >= hint_delay_sec` and `input_seen` is false. No e13 probe def sets `hint_delay_sec`, so nothing ships broken, but the first e14 def that does will show the drag hint over a child who is tapping.
- Fix: have the templates call a `TemplateContext` callback, or have `ScenePlayer` connect the tray, pad and tool signals to `_note_input`.

### A13 (consider): `t25_map` accepts pins at the world edge, and `t50_lanes` ignores a resize

- Evidence:
  - `step_params.gd:133` accepts any point inside `Rect2(ZERO, world_size)`, but a pin is 120 px wide. A pin at (3999, 400) in a 4000 px world shows 1 px at the maximum pan. `t25_map.reachable` (`:134-140`) accepts `at + (1, 1)` as reachable, which has the same low bar. Use `Rect2(ZERO, size - TapButton.MIN_HIT_SIZE)`.
  - `t50_lanes.gd:24` reads the host size once in `setup` and never listens for `resized`, while `t25_map` re-reads it (`_on_resized`, `t25_map.gd:233`). A rotation or window change leaves the lane bounds stale.
  - `RubSurface._release_layer` (`rub_surface.gd:98`) leaves a `leave`-mode layer (soap) on the screen if the next step is not a rub step that claims it; it lingers until the room exits.
- Fix: one-line bound change in `_map`; re-read `_field_size()` on `resized` in t50; free a `KEPT` layer when the next step does not claim it.

## What I checked and found sound

- Size bars: all changed `.gd` files are at most 299 lines and no function exceeds 20 non-comment lines (script check over every added or modified `.gd` file in the range). `progress_manager.gd` at 299 stays under the bar.
- Strict typing (type gate 263/0), `gdlint`, `gdformat --check`, `# story:` tags (`trace-stories.sh --strict`).

The map and lane e2e tests inject echoes and second fingers. One-finger ownership in `PanPrimitive` and `SteerPrimitive` (both `Fsm`-based with named `STATE_*`) holds, including the second-finger lift not cancelling the first finger's tap.

- Spawner and lanes (t50): seeded and deterministic (`Spawner`), a miss is quiet, there are no lives or shake, rows are freed on catch, miss and stop, and teardown removes the pad and field (e2e `before - 2`).
