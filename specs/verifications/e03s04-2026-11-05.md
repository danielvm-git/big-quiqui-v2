## Defects found

1. **One real mouse click fired twice.** Found by the orchestrator's AC 15 run. `TapButton` and `QuiQuiRig` filtered device -1 only on mouse events, so the emulated touch from `emulate_touch_from_mouse` counted as a second tap. Fixed with a failing test first. `TouchDraggable` already handled this path, and a new test pins that. Not added to `specs/bugs/registry.yaml`, as this story's brief carries the fix.

No other defect was found.
