## Gap closed after the fix commit (F2 acceptance criterion)

The audit's F2 remedy was *"record an ADR superseding ADR-002 and update `DESIGN.md`"*. ADR-008 was
written, but ADR-002 still carried the `SafeStage` MUST and `DESIGN.md` §3 still offered two
equivalent options — so the decision records contradicted the shipped code, and e03 would have
inherited the contradiction. Closed in `docs(adr)`: ADR-002's composition clause is marked superseded,
`DESIGN.md` §3 names one composition rule (ratio/edge anchoring, no `SafeStage` node), and ADR-008's
`Supersedes` field says what it actually replaces.
