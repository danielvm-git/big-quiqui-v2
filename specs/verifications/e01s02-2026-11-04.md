## Behaviour proven

**SceneManager**
- declares `IDLE → FADING_OUT → SWAPPING → FADING_IN → IDLE`; an undeclared edge is impossible by construction and `FsmProbe` stays clean
- the loader is called exactly once per transition, and only after the fade-out completes
- the 1-slot queue keeps the **latest** request: `A, B, C` loads `A` then `C`; a repeated pending request is not queued twice
- same-scene and empty-path requests are rejected with a typed reason, not silently ignored
- a stuck fade (10 s configured) is recovered to `IDLE` at 2.0 s, and the parked scene starts immediately after recovery
- `reset()` returns a busy manager to `IDLE` (this is what `TestReset` calls)

**AudioManager**
- `Music`, `SFX`, `Voice` exist and send to `Master`
- SFX pool has exactly 8 players and round-robins (`0..7`, then `0` again)
- ducking moves Music and SFX by exactly −9 dB, never Voice, is idempotent, and restores the **exact** previous value
- a voice line docks on play and restores on `finished`
- missing streams return `false`/`-1` and log — never crash
- the music crossfade completes at 0.4 s and lands on `music_db`

## Determinism notes

Both managers take `delta` explicitly (`advance()`), and both expose `manual_step` so
`_process` cannot outrun a stepping test. `SceneManager` also feeds the FSM a
test-stepped clock, so `advance(2.1)` means the same thing as 2.1 s of wall time —
the watchdog is not time-flaky in CI.

## Test-harness fix found here

Connecting signal spies inside `before_each` stacked them across tests (the autoload
outlives the test), so `queued_signals` grew to nine entries. Spies now connect once in
`before_all` and only the arrays are cleared per test.
