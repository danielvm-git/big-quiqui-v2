## Behaviour proven

- **WishBubble:** hidden and touch-transparent to start; `show_wish("res://…/carrot.png")` reveals the bubble with the asked icon; a second wish swaps the icon without hiding; `hide_wish()` closes it; missing art keeps it hidden (graceful degradation, `ui.missing_art` logged); the pulse is a deterministic function of accumulated `advance(delta)` time — 1.0 while hidden, within ±4% while shown, and replaying the same timeline reproduces the exact value.
- **CareDotsBar:** exactly five dots; `set_completed_steps(3)` fills exactly the first three with `progress_dot.png`, the rest stay `progress_dot_empty.png`; counts clamp into 0..5; only the two declared textures ever appear.
- **CoinTicketHud:** 0/0 on a fresh save; `refresh()` reads the real `ProgressManager.data` (typed `SaveData` cast); `pm.add_coins(3)` updates the HUD through the `progress_changed` signal with no manual refresh; a missing manager degrades to zeros without crashing (`manager_path` seam).

## Defects found by the tests

1. `WishBubble.hide()` shadowed the native `CanvasItem.hide()` — the test script failed to parse ("Could not resolve external class member"). Renamed to `hide_wish()` to pair with `show_wish()`.
2. gdlint max-line-length (120) on the HUD `_exit_tree` guard — fixed by `gdformat` (wrapped boolean chain).

## Deviations recorded

- **Ticket glyph:** no `bilhete.png` art exists in the 98-asset set. Per user decision (2026-11-04) the ticket counter uses `items/fortune_wheel.png` — the wheel is what the ticket is for. Swap point is a single constant (`TICKET_ICON_PATH`) when art lands.
- **Numerals:** counts render as default-font numerals with a chocolate outline (#4A2511, DESIGN.md line-art token) next to icons — decoration, not reading (Zero Text Rule upheld). Glossy font polish deferred with the art pass.
