## Verified clean

- **Export:** `game/export_presets.cfg` exists, carries the bundle id and the App Store team id, and
  the app excludes `tests/`; `scripts/ios-deploy.sh` prints no secrets or environment
  (`grep -nE "password|secret|token|BEGIN"` → nothing), and CI's `export` job builds an artifact.
- **Save backup:** the `.bak` is written on a good save, recovered on a corrupt one, and is
  redirected in test mode (`test_save_backup.gd`, `test_persistence_invariants.gd`).
- **Dev console:** compiled out of a release build, asserted by `test_dev_console.gd`.
- **Invariant gate:** every layer can fail (INV-08 drives each oracle with a broken input).
- **Budget:** 76 scripts / 599 tests / 8786 asserts, Preflight green with the tag present.

## Outcome

Eight findings, six fixed in this pass, two documented. The epic's remaining gap is not code: the
**v1.0.0 breaking release** is a human decision, and no agent run is approved to take it.
