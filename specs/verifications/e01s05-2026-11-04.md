## The slice

`res://scenes/titulo.tscn` — the main scene of the project:

| Node | Role |
|---|---|
| `Titulo` (Control, script `titulo.gd`) | root, `PRESET_FULL_RECT` |
| `Background` (TextureRect, `bg_title.jpg`) | full-bleed `EXPAND_IGNORE_SIZE` + `KEEP_ASPECT_COVERED`, group `background` |
| `QuiQuiRig` (Control + `quiqui_rig.gd`) | 700×620, breathing + blinking; tapping her makes her laugh (`rindo`) |
| `PlayButton` (Control + `tap_button.gd`) | 168×168, `play_button.png`, group `hit_target` |

The scene file is generated by `game/tools/build_titulo_scene.gd`, which builds the tree
and saves `PackedScene.pack(...)` — Godot authors the `.tscn`, so UIDs and layout flags are
correct by construction instead of hand-repaired.
