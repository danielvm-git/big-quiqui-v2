## Summary

The logic layer is good. The primitives, the step-param grammar, the ledgers, the reward guard and the
coverage gate are all careful work. Every input path I traced is **reachable**: no STOP control sits
above a target's centre at any canvas. But the real player **draws** QuiQui and the Home button above
almost every surface e13 adds, so what a child sees does not match what a finger can reach:

- QuiQui's sprites have `z_index` 10–50 (the full-body art is on `Expression`, z 20). Godot draws higher
  z above lower z, whatever the tree order, and every e13 control is at z 0. The rub layers that t34, t23
  and t40 lay over her are **completely invisible**. The title verb, the second tray target, the dress
  item, the map's bath door and the middle lane all sit inside her rect and draw behind her.
- The Home button's `Art` is a 1024×1024 `TextureRect` with no `expand_mode`, so it paints a yellow
  disc over roughly x 36–1060, y 36–1060 at every canvas. It hides the first tray slot (apple, star,
  plush, rub tool at 16:9), the map's first door and the lane-0 basket.

The reach gates (`test_template_aspects`, `test_tray_reach_e2e`) cannot catch either problem, because
`HitProbe` models input only and ignores draw order. Preflight is green, but the child-facing result
fails three acceptance criteria.

## Findings

### Must-fix

**A1 — Rub layers are invisible: QuiQui's z 10–50 sprites cover every layer t34, t23 and t40 draw on her.**
- Evidence: `game/scripts/components/quiqui_rig.gd:21-26` (`Z_OUTFIT=10`, `Z_EXPRESSION=20`, … `Z_GLASSES=50`)
  and `:341-345,355-360` (`sprite.z_index = layer`, z relative to a z-0 Control). The full-body pose
  texture is on `expression` (`:365-372`, `_update_layout` sizes the rig from it).
  `game/scripts/components/rub_surface.gd:159-166` adds each layer as a z-0 Control and moves it to
  `rig.get_index() + 1`. That fixes the tree order, but tree order does not beat z_index.
- Proof (render probe): `rub_reveal_probe` at 1920×1080 with the rig visible shows no crumbs and no
  foam anywhere on QuiQui. With `QuiQuiRig.visible = false` and nothing else changed, the crumbs and the
  foam bubble appear exactly over her rect (layer global rect `(812.9, 222.4) 294×620`, alpha 1.0,
  index 4 vs rig index 2).
- Breaks: e13s01 AC3/AC5 ("each layer animates away as it is rubbed"; verification step 3). e13s04
  AC3/AC6 and verification steps 3–5 ("the foam disappears"). The `leave` soap layer that t40 paints is
  also behind her, so the child never sees the soap that the rinse step clears.
- Fix: draw rub layers above the character. For example, set `layer.z_index = QuiQuiRig.Z_GLASSES + 1`
  in `RubSurface._make_layer`, or give the rig a `CanvasGroup`/`z_as_relative` scheme that keeps the
  character at z 0 and orders the layers by pivot child order. Add a render-order assertion: the
  effective z of every owned layer is greater than the max z of the rig's visible sprites.

**A2 — Tap targets, map pins and the middle lane are drawn behind QuiQui.**
- Same root cause as A1 (rig sprites at z ≥ 10, e13 controls at z 0), together with placements inside
  her rect. QuiQui's visible box from the probe: **1920×1080** x 825–1095, y 234–830.
  **1920×1440** x 776–1144, y 309–1111.
  - `t0_title.gd:85`: the verb goes at centre + 0.2 H, i.e. `(840,636) 240²` at 1080 (194 of 240 px of
    height inside her box) and `(840,888)` at 1440 (222 of 240 px inside). The render shows only a pink
    sliver between her feet. The **one verb of the title screen is hidden**.
  - `t21_touch_each.gd:83` and `t51_prize_pick.gd:106` (row at `160·i` from tray x 650): slot 1
    `(810–930, 614–734)` is 105 of 120 px behind her at 1080 and fully inside her at 1440. The render
    shows neither grape (`touch_each_probe`) nor pinwheel (`prize_probe`).
  - `t45_dress_decorate.gd:190-199`: the scarf at `794–914` is 89 of 120 px behind her at 1080 and fully
    behind her at 1440.
  - `home_map.tres` (`bath_door` at world (800,400)) and `t25_map.gd:174-201` (the world is mounted at
    slot 1, under the rig): the bath door is fully inside her box at 1440 and 96 of 120 px inside at
    1080. Not visible in the render.
  - `t50_lanes.gd:183-190`: with 3 lanes the middle lane is x 912–1008, inside her box for most of the
    fall. The render shows a strawberry only above her head.
- Breaks: e13s05 AC3 ("one glowing target starts the game"), e13s02 AC4 (each object answers; a child
  cannot see the object to touch), e13s03 AC7 / verification step 5 ("the same destinations are
  reachable" at 4:3), e13s08 verification step 3.
- Fix: same z fix as A1 for the character, and lay e13 surfaces out of her rect. The tray row should go
  below her feet or start beside her. The title verb should sit beside her, or the rig should be hidden
  for t0/t25. StepParams `_pin_fits` should reject pins that overlap the rig's box at the def's start
  offset. Extend `test_template_aspects` with an occlusion check (see A4).

**A3 — The Home button paints a 1024 px disc over the left half of the player.**
- Evidence: `game/tools/build_scene_player_scene.gd:130-135` creates `HomeButton/Visual/Art` with no
  `expand_mode`. The default `EXPAND_KEEP_SIZE` makes its minimum size the texture size
  (`assets/art/ui/home_button.png` is 1024×1024), so it ignores the 148 px button. Compare `:63`, where
  the tray art sets `EXPAND_IGNORE_SIZE`. The generated `game/scenes/scene_player.tscn` has the same
  node without `expand_mode`.
- Proof (render probe, every def, both canvases): a yellow Home disc covers about x 45–840, y 45–960 (at
  1080). Hidden or mostly hidden under it: the first tray slot at `(650–770, 614–734)` (apple, reward
  star, plush, the rub tool at 16:9), `feed_door` at `(100–220, 400–520)` (only a sliver of the apple
  pin shows), and the lane-0 basket at `(260–380, 804–924)`. The `HitProbe` says all of these "answer",
  because `Art` is `IGNORE`.
- The builder predates e13, but every e13 template mounts in this player and is defeated by it. The
  "Never dismiss as pre-existing" rule applies.
- Fix: in the builder, set `art.expand_mode = TextureRect.EXPAND_IGNORE_SIZE` and
  `art.stretch_mode = TextureRect.STRETCH_KEEP_ASPECT_CENTERED`, then regenerate `scene_player.tscn` and
  commit both. Add a geometry assertion that every `Visual/Art` rect stays inside its button rect.

### Should-fix

**A4 — The reach gates cannot fail on visual occlusion, and `HitProbe` is less faithful than its doc says.**
- `game/tests/helpers/hit_probe.gd:1-28` walks tree order and `mouse_filter` only. That is correct for
  input, but `test_template_aspects.gd:2-5` claims each target "is on screen and nothing above it takes
  the touch", and nothing checks that it is *seen*. A1–A3 pass both gates.
- The helper also differs from Godot's `Viewport::_gui_find_control_at_pos`. It descends into
  non-`CanvasItem` children, which Godot skips (a Control under the `HintManager` Node would wrongly
  "answer"). It ignores `top_level`, `CanvasLayer` order (`SceneCurtain`, `DevConsole`) and `_has_point`
  overrides. No shipped target hits these today.
- Fix: add `HitProbe.drawn_over(root, target)`. It walks visible `CanvasItem`s with effective z ≥ the
  target's, in draw order, and reports any with an opaque rect over the target centre (use
  `QuiQuiRig.get_visual_global_rect()` for the rig and the texture rect for a `TextureRect`). Assert it
  is empty in both gates. Skip non-CanvasItem and `top_level` children to match the engine.

**A5 — The title screen has two interactive targets.**
- `t0_title.gd:69-75` quiets every host child **except Home**. `test_surfaces_player_e2e.gd:18` asserts
  `live == 2`. Home on this def also exits to `next_scene_id = "home"` (`scene_player.gd:124-141`), so
  two targets do the same thing. The unit test `test_t0_title.gd:6-17` passes only because its bare host
  has no Home.
- Contradicts e13s05 AC3 and the frozen contract "Anything else on that screen is decoration with
  `MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE`", and SC-e13s05-P1-03 "exactly one interactive target".
- Fix: quiet Home in t0 (restore it in teardown) and assert `live == 1` in the e2e test. Or amend the
  story and test plan to "one verb plus the Home chrome" and say why.

**A6 — On a return visit, tapping the piece she already wears does nothing: no sound, no progress.**
- `t45_dress_decorate.gd:93-106`: `before == item_id` gives `changed = false`, so `_count_change` returns
  before `play_hit`. `progress()` is `_changes / repeat_count` (`:49-50`). With `dress_probe`
  (one item), a child who comes back with the scarf on taps it and gets only a squash. The step finishes
  only if she finds the bin and then taps the scarf again (the bin `:127-136` does not count either).
  "A dead control teaches a child that touching does not work" (e13s02 frozen contract).
- Fix: always play the hit reaction on a successful `wear`. Count "the look is confirmed" (a tap on a
  worn piece) as completing the step, or seed `_changes` from the saved look on setup. Add a re-entry
  test.

**A7 — The shipped t21 def cannot show "touch each".**
- `game/data/scenes/touch_each_probe.tres` uses `mode = "any"`. The first tap sets progress to 1
  (`tap_primitive.gd:33`), and the single-step def goes STEP_DONE → OUTRO → EXIT within about 0.05 s
  (`step_runner.gd:157-159,175-177`). The e13s02 verification script steps 3–4 ("the step completes only
  when all counted ones are touched"; "tap a spent object → it still reacts") cannot be performed through
  the player. The spent-replay rule is proven only in a unit test.
- Fix: ship the probe as `mode = "count_n"` with `repeat_count = 2`, and add an e2e test that taps the
  same object twice through `finger_tap` and asserts the step is still live.

**A8 — The "scrolling category strip" has no finger-scroll proof.**
- `t45_dress_decorate.gd:186-199` builds a `ScrollContainer` of STOP `TapButton`s, and StepParams allows
  40 items (`step_params.gd:16`). The only scroll in any test is the programmatic
  `ensure_control_visible` (`test_template_aspects.gd:80-82`). No test shows that a finger drag scrolls
  the strip while its children are STOP buttons. The probe def has one item, so it never needs to
  scroll.
- Fix: add a dress def with more items than fit the strip, plus a test that drags across an item and
  asserts `scroll_horizontal` changed and the item did not fire. If Godot's touch-drag is blocked by STOP
  children, give the strip its own pan (e.g. `PanPrimitive` with a tap tolerance, as t25 does).

### Consider (not scored)

- **C1** At 2048×1536 the right 20 px of the rub tool `(714–834)` is under QuiQui's STOP rect (left
  edge 814.7). Rub templates do not quiet the rig. Probe corners: `["ok","QuiQuiRig","ok","QuiQuiRig"]`.
  The centre reaches the tool.
- **C2** `t32_reward.gd:56-64`: outside CELEBRATE, `collect_reward()` returns false, yet the star goes
  LIT → DONE and the step completes with no gift and no coin flight. On re-entry the star is unlit again.
  Consider staying UNLIT with a soft miss.
- **C3** One-step defs exit about 0.05 s after the last tap, so the hit pose and SFX are cut off by the
  scene change. A short OUTRO dwell would let the reaction land.
- **C4** The dress tab art (`category_ids = "warm"`) has no picture. It falls back to the star and writes
  `item.missing_art` on every mount. StepParams could require art for tab ids.
- **C5** `EXPLICIT_STATE_MACHINES.md` does not list the new machines (Pan, Steer, t32, t51). It was
  already behind before e13.
- **C6** Tray rows are at tray y 0, above the visible tray panel (`stretch_mode = keep aspect centered`
  in a 620×620 zone), so the row floats over QuiQui's feet rather than on the tray.
- **C7** `TouchPad` ignores `InputEventScreenTouch.canceled`. A system gesture mid-drag can leave
  Steer/Pan owned until the next lift.

## What I checked and found sound

- **Preflight**: green end to end (engine import, type gate, 829 tests, diary audit, trace, traceability,
  specs schema, version, CI lint, release config).
- **Input reach** (my own probe, 3 canvases × 9 defs, centre plus corners): every tray `TapButton`,
  the title verb, the dress strip items, the bin, the reward star, the prizes and the Home button answer
  a finger. The map and lane pads take the stage. Rig quieting and restore are correct in t21/t32/t45/
  t50/t51/t25.
- **Tap primitive**: `count_n` counts distinct targets, capped by the target count. `toggle` is done when
  all are on. Spent taps still react. No timer anywhere. Echo filtering is inherited from `TapButton`.
- **Rub primitive**: lazy loop start, hull read at call time, cell ratio with the same hull mask in the
  numerator and the denominator, `release_all` frees the loop. CleaningRub latches at the def threshold
  and cannot un-complete. The tray holds during a gesture and comes back afterwards.
- **t34**: reveals in def order and never reverses (`_gone` latch).
- **Pan**: clamp, tolerance, HELD-on-target (never pans under a pin), single owner finger, the second
  finger's lift cannot cancel the first. Resize re-clamps.
- **t25**: locks read `CareLoopManager.completed_steps` only, with no wallet calls. Destinations resolve
  through `SceneCatalog.is_shipped` in StepParams and again at tap time, and `SceneNav` never builds a
  path from def text. A refusal gives a soft SFX plus a wish bubble and no text.
- **Steer and Spawner**: lanes snap only on the owner's lift. The spawner is seeded with no wall clock
  and has a second-consumer stub. Rows and nodes are freed on catch, miss and stop. There is no
  life/shake/projectile anywhere.
- **t32 and CareLoopManager**: payment stays at CELEBRATE entry (`_grant_cycle_reward` keyed by
  `cycle_id`). `collect_reward` marks collected once per cycle, and a re-entry tap never pays. The gift
  goes through `buy_furniture(id, 0)` against the catalogue.
- **t51 and PrizeLedger**: one pick per `completed_cycles`, ids validated, a re-entry shows the saved
  pick, a second tap only carries on, no ad branch.
- **t45**: `wear` admits only `OutfitCatalog` pieces in their own slot. Furniture placement requires
  ownership. The bin clears through `clear_slot` with a soft sound and no dialog.
- **SceneDefLoader and StepParams**: closed per-template checks (modes, counts, thresholds, world and
  pin bounds, locks in `CareSteps`, unique ids, voice keys resolved, reserved node ids refused for
  layers). A bad def is rejected before mount.
- **ProgressManager and SaveData**: the atomic `.tmp` write is intact. `_commit` keeps the "failed write
  still repaints" rule. The new `picked_prize`/`prize_cycle` fields have defaults and are cleaned on read
  (ADR-005 "adding a field"). The ledgers are pure.
- **Coverage gate**: `TemplateTableGate` reads the table, the template scripts and tests on disk, the
  consuming def and the scope claim. Negative tests prove that it fails on a missing row, a blank cell, a
  duplicate, a def without the template, and a scope mismatch.
- **Conventions**: typed throughout (type gate). Every changed `.gd` has a `# story:` tag. No changed
  file is over 300 lines and no function over 20 (my own scan of every changed `.gd`). The state
  machines use `Fsm` with no flag clusters. `device == -1` is filtered in `TouchPad`, `TapButton` and
  `ScenePlayer._input`. No `HTTPRequest`/`HTTPClient`/`WebSocketPeer`/ads/telemetry. `scene_player.tscn`
  is unchanged in the range. The `.import` changes are engine re-imports.

## Verdict

**FAIL** — must_fix = 3 (> 0) and score 79.5 < 94.

Preflight is green and the input logic holds. But through the real ScenePlayer a child cannot see the
rub layers at all, and cannot see the title verb, several tray targets, the map's doors or the middle
and left lanes. The cause is QuiQui's z-ordered sprites and an unsized Home art. Neither reach gate can
detect this.
