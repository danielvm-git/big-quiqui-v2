## Behaviour proven

- The full legal walk `IDLE→FEED→BATH→DRESS→PLAY→SLEEP→CELEBRATE→IDLE` transitions exactly once per call, verified by the `loop_state_changed` signal sequence.
- Illegal entry (`begin_step("BATH")` from IDLE), skipped steps, and stale duplicate `complete_step` calls are all rejected without moving the machine (`transition_from` stale guard, invariant 1).
- **Detour guard (invariant 2):** entering CELEBRATE pays `+5 coins / +1 ticket` via `ProgressManager.complete_cycle()` exactly once per `cycle_id`; leaving CELEBRATE to IDLE — by completion **or** by `abandon_step()` detour — closes the cycle (`cycle_id += 1`) without a second payment and without soft-locking (`begin_step("FEED")` works immediately after). A second full cycle pays again (`5/1 → 10/2`, `completed_cycles == 2`).
- `abandon_step()` returns any step to IDLE; dots read `completed_steps() == 0` after an abandon (cycle progress is FSM state, not persisted data — by design).
- `TestReset.reset_all()` reaches the 5th autoload (registered in `project.godot` after `SceneManager`); `reset()` restores a fresh cycle.

## Control (anti-vacuity)

The reward tests assert exact coin/ticket counts through the real `ProgressManager` under `user://test_progress.json` isolation (`TestReset`), not through mocks — the economy invariant is proven end-to-end at the autoload boundary.

The file keeps its e01s01 tag; the change ships in the e02s01 commit and is recorded here.

## Deviations recorded

- Care states carry **no dwell limits** (EXPLICIT_STATE_MACHINES §2 defines none): a child may take unbounded time inside a step. `Fsm` was built with an empty dwell table on purpose; `FsmProbe` therefore can never see a dwell timeout on this machine.
- `set_test_mode(true)` only records the flag: the manager writes nothing itself — `ProgressManager` owns the test-path redirect, so duplicate machinery here would be dead code.
